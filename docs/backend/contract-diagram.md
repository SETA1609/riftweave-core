## Session steps

What each session step reads from the stored actor and writes back, from the table in
[sessions.md](sessions.md#what-a-step-reads-and-writes).

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    actor[("stored actor")]
    derived["derived"]
    actor -- "fills: actor, modifiers (the actor's, below), and templates (the held character templates, when any)" --> derived
    derived -- "writes back: nothing" --> actor
    combat["combat"]
    actor -- "fills: attacker.attributes, attacker.skill (the equipped weapon's skill), and modifiers (the actor's on that skill); or with as: defender defender.hp, defender.attributes, defender.armor_dr (the worn armor), and defender.modifiers (the actor's)" --> combat
    combat -- "writes back: with as: defender, HP" --> actor
    rest["rest"]
    actor -- "fills: actor, current, max.stamina" --> rest
    rest -- "writes back: HP, MP, SP, AP, and any condition the rest cleared" --> actor
    casting["casting"]
    actor -- "fills: caster.skills, caster.attributes, caster.mana, modifiers (the actor's)" --> casting
    casting -- "writes back: MP" --> actor
    progression["progression"]
    actor -- "fills: actor for level_up; for spend with key, skill (the skill's value, as read_skill reads it) and tagged (whether key is a tag); for take_perk, actor and perks" --> progression
    progression -- "writes back: level sets XP and level; level_up adds its rolls to growth; spend with key adds the bought ranks to that skill; take_perk sets the perk to its new rank when taken" --> actor
    spellcraft["spellcraft"]
    actor -- "fills: for learn, caster.known (the effects the actor knows)" --> spellcraft
    spellcraft -- "writes back: learn adds the spell to spells and sets known_effects to known" --> actor
    consume["consume"]
    actor -- "fills: current, max" --> consume
    consume -- "writes back: HP, MP, SP" --> actor
    conditions["conditions"]
    actor -- "fills: conditions" --> conditions
    conditions -- "writes back: the conditions and flags" --> actor
    dev["dev"]
    actor -- "fills: nothing" --> dev
    dev -- "writes back: what the operation names (dev-mode.md); only with --dev" --> actor
```
