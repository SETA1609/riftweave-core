#### M3-16 Natural weapons are unarmed attacks
- Status: todo
- Priority: medium
- Needs: M3-10
- Do: Owner question, 2026-11-12: whether monsters without weapons should just make unarmed attacks with feats. Decision: keep the `natural_weapons` table, because a bite, a gore, and a slam differ in dice and damage type and change with creature size, and a feat per attack type would be the same table again. But every natural weapon is an unarmed attack: it rolls to hit with the `unarmed` skill, and the unarmed perks (`unarmed_damage`, `unarmed_strike_damage`, and the rest) apply to it. A creature with no attack listed attacks with `core:natural_weapon/slam` [A]. A race's natural weapon names a natural weapon row instead of writing its own dice: the feature field `natural_weapon` becomes a `core:natural_weapon/<key>` ref (`catman_claws` names `claw`), so a catfolk and a jaguar use one rule, sized by the actor's size **(breaking)**. Decide whether a player's natural weapon takes the creature size steps or only its race's size [A].
