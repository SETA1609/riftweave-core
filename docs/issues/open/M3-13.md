#### M3-13 Charisma strengthens summons through a perk
- Status: doing
- Priority: low
- Needs: M3-10, M3-12
- Do: Owner request, 2026-11-11: CHA should help summoning. A conjuration perk adds the caster's CHA modifier (`CHA − 5`, M3-12) to a summoned creature's attributes when it is summoned, through the same merge as the power and nature templates (M3-10). The caster picks one group when taking the perk: magic (INT, WIL) or physical (STR, END, AGI) [A]; a higher rank or a second perk could add the other group [A]. Without the perk CHA changes nothing, and a CHA below 5 gives no penalty to the creature [A]. The creature's HP, mana, and attack damage then follow its new attributes as M3-10 defines. Write how a player and a GM read it in `docs/guide/` too.
- Keys: the conjuration perk or perks in `features/core.json`; the group it grants (`magic` or `physical`)
- Files: ~6 (docs/modules/magic.md, docs/modules/monsters.md, docs/guide/quick-start.md, ruleset/data/features/core.json, ruleset/scripts/test_spells.py, ruleset/tests/vectors/)
- See: docs/issues/open/M3-14.md (the same bonus for companions and the party), docs/issues/open/M3-10.md, docs/modules/attributes.md#the-attribute-modifier
- Done when: a summon from a caster with the perk and CHA 8 gets +3 on each attribute of the chosen group and nothing on the other; without the perk, or with CHA 5 or below, the creature is unchanged; `validate.py` and the tests pass.
