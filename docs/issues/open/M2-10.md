#### M2-10 A rest ends food buffs unless you eat during it
- Status: todo
- Priority: low
- Needs: M2-09
- Do: Owner rule, 2026-11-12: a rest ends every running food effect, the effects whose source is `food:<key>` ([modifier.md](../../core/modifier.md#origin)). This keeps resting from being free: a player who camps loses the meal's buff unless they eat during the rest. They can eat the same dish again to keep the buff or pick another one, and either way it costs a ration. Model it in the rest table, not as code: each rest row gets a `food` line with a mode such as `clear` [A], beside the existing resources and `conditions`. The `rest` function takes an optional `meal`, a `core:consumable/<key>` with `delivery` `eat` and `type: food`. The rest clears food effects first, then applies the meal, so the meal's effects run from the end of the rest. Food is never eaten in combat (M2-07): `consume` refuses a `type: food` item while in combat, so no one eats a meal mid-fight. The food effects a fight can hold all come from a dish eaten before it. `end_of_combat` keeps those effects (`none`), since a fight is not a rest: they run until their duration ends or a rest clears them [V owner 2026-11-12]. **At a table:** after a fight, the GM may resolve what is left of a food effect from that earlier meal at once, as its remaining total, when no other encounter is near or whenever they choose [V owner 2026-11-12]. Write this in `docs/core/rest.md` and the guide as a table shortcut; the vectors keep the per-second rule. A meal eaten at rest also counts for the survival module's hunger meter, through that module's normal nutrition table, once the module exists [V owner 2026-11-12]. A new dish still replaces the last one's effects (M2-07). Today every stock dish lasts 60 seconds, so this changes nothing in play until dishes give longer buffs. Write the rule now so longer dishes have it from the start.
