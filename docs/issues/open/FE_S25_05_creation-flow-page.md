- Do: Owner request, 2026-11-24: creation is a page flow, one step after the other or in groups, in an order that makes sense, and the character is saved at the end. It replaces the one-page CV form of the dropped FE_S07_09_character-creation-cv-form.
  - **The flow** is the core's: the page reads creation `rules.flow` and draws its steps in that order, grouped as it says (a wide screen shows a group on one page; a phone, one step) [A]. A progress bar names each step and marks it done, with problems, or still to do, from `check`; a done step can be opened again.
  - **Next and Back:** Next goes to `check`'s `next` and is disabled while the open step has problems; Back goes to the step before. Every change runs `check`, so the sheet in the side column (attributes, skills, derived stats) is always the core's.
  - **The steps:** who (name, and male or female from `creation.sexes`; no sex choice for a race that reads `none`, which shows "it"); race; background; traits (creation perks, with their trade-offs); attributes (only − and + on each, the `rw-point-stepper`: + stops at no points left or the attribute's maximum, − at the minimum); tag skills; perks (the starting perk, only those whose prerequisites the draft meets); review (the whole sheet as the character list will show it).
  - **Choices from data:** races, backgrounds, traits, skills, and perks come from `creation rules`, each with its description, and link to its compendium entry when that exists (FE_S08_11_compendium-tables-page).
  - **Saved:** Finish saves the character to the character list (IndexedDB) and offers "Play as this character", which makes it the profile (FE_S25_07_current-character-profile-page). The draft is kept in IndexedDB, so a reload or Back to the menu does not lose it, and Back asks before dropping it. The page shows no JSON; Export downloads the character file.
  - **Components:** small ones in `frontend/src/app/ui/` (`rw-step-progress`, `rw-point-stepper`, a choice list with descriptions), signals, `ng generate`, our own CSS with Bootstrap's look (docs/backend/frontend.md#conventions).
