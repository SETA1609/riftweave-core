#### M5-47 Suggested builds: three paths, nine sub-paths, three hybrids
- Status: todo

- Do: Owner request, 2026-11-12: the system is classless, so a new player needs worked examples of how to reach a style of play. Add **suggested builds**, not classes. Every build and every page says it is a suggestion. No mechanic is gated by a build: any character can use any weapon, spell, or skill, and leaving the build is only less efficient, never forbidden. For example, a blaster mage with good AGI and evasion can still fight with a dagger at close range, or hit hard with a staff if their STR is high enough.
  - **What a build holds:** level 1 is creation perks and traits (`creation` features), as in `examples/characters.json` [V owner 2026-11-12]. From level 2 to `max_level` (30), a suggested perk for every level that grants one. It also suggests tag skills, an attribute spread, and where the skill points go.
  - **The twelve builds:** three main paths, **melee**, **magic**, and **ranged**, each with three sub-paths, for nine builds. The spec step picks the sub-paths, for example melee: shield-bearer, two-handed, dual-wield; magic: blaster, summoner, support; ranged: bow, crossbow, firearm or thrown [A]. Beside the nine come three **hybrids**: a magic swordsman (melee + magic), an arcane archer or trickster (ranged + magic), and a ranger (melee + ranged).
  - **Docs:** one main page, `docs/modules/builds/README.md`. It explains what a suggested build is and that nothing is gated, lists the twelve builds, and shows how to read a build and how to leave one. Each build also gets its own page: the mechanics it relies on (the skills, attributes, perks, and spells or weapons, with links to their module docs), how a player reaches that style of play level by level, and what a player of that build can still do outside it, at what cost.
  - **Data:** each build names only existing `core:feature/...` perks, so `validate.py` checks every reference. Decide whether builds extend `examples/characters.json` (today only the `martial` and `caster` concepts, with full level logs) or become a new `builds` collection with its own schema, since a build is a plan, not a played character [A: a new collection, because it has no luck rolls and no gear log].
  - **Gaps:** where a path has no perk worth taking at some level, list it as a gap in `docs/decisions/M5-47.md` instead of inventing a perk in this issue.
