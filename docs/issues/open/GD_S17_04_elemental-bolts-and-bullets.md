#### GD_S17_04_elemental-bolts-and-bullets Elemental bolts and bullets: a grenade's burst at the weapon's damage

- Do: Owner request, 2026-11-18: crossbows and guns get elemental bolts and bullets that work like grenades, but with the weapon's bonuses in place of the grenade's fixed damage.
  - **The shot:** an elemental round is fired as a normal `single` attack at one target, with the weapon's skill, range, and every attack modifier. On a hit, the target takes the weapon's damage as the round's element instead of `piercing`.
  - **The burst:** hit or miss, the round bursts at the target, in the matching grenade's radius (GD_S17_03, radius 1 [A]). Every other creature in the radius takes the area roll and the **same damage roll** as the target: the weapon's dice plus every weapon bonus (material, enchantments, damage perks, and a critical strike's multiplier), as the element. On a miss the target is in the burst too and takes the area roll, like any other creature in it [A]. The round's rider (`burn`, `slow`, `stagger`, `sunder_armor`) applies to every creature the burst hits, as a grenade's does.
  - **Which weapons:** bolts for every crossbow and bullets for every gun. Blunderbuss scatter charges burst along its cone instead of a radius, decided with the cone in GD_S17_08 [A]. Bows get no elemental arrows in core [A]: the bow line stays the precise single-target one, so the paths differ.
  - **Rows** (names our own) [A]: Fire, Frost, Thunder, and Acid bolts, and the same four bullets, each in bundles of 5, at about three times the matching grenade's cost per round, and from merchant and loot level 6 up, so they come later in a character's life than plain rounds [A].
  - **Phase:** an elemental round gives the shot the element's phase for the five-phase cycles (the wuxing module), as a coating does for a bound weapon (GD_S06_01).
  - **One rule, written once:** the burst is a rule of the ammunition row (radius, element, rider), read by the attack pipeline, not a special case per weapon, so the Bound Ammunition of GD_S17_11 and a module's rounds reuse it.
- Keys: the burst fields on ammunition rows; `fire_bolt_round`, `frost_bolt_round`, `thunder_bolt_round`, `acid_bolt_round`, and the four `*_bullet` rows [A, names fixed in the spec step]
