#### GD_S05_02_fighting-stances Fighting stances: toggles a perk unlocks

- Do: Owner request, 2026-11-16: a stance is a toggle, not a passive. M3-27 makes block and dodge declared stances; this issue adds the stances a perk unlocks, each tied to what the actor holds.
  - **The rule:** an actor has at most one fighting stance active [A]. Taking one at the start of the actor's own turn costs nothing; changing it mid-turn costs 1 AP [A]. It lasts until changed. If the loadout stops matching (a weapon is dropped or swapped), the stance ends. Its modifiers carry the `martial` origin and the source `stance:<key>`, `while_applied` (docs/core/modifier.md). Whether a fighting stance can stand together with M3-27's block or dodge stance is decided in the spec step.
  - **Loadout:** each stance names what the hands must hold: the main-hand skill, and the off hand as `empty`, `shield`, `buckler`, `light_weapon`, `weapon`, or `two_handed` [A]. The same loadout terms as M3-28's `attack_sequences`, written once in `schema.json`.
  - **The stances** (names original, owner 2026-11-16; a perk unlocks each):
    - `fencers_measure` (Fencer's Measure; replaces `duelist`, Lv12): one-handed blade, off hand empty. The weapon skill feeds a parry band, `floor(weapon / 4)` [A], and +10 to attack when only one foe is in reach.
    - `espada_y_daga` (Espada y Daga, Lv8): blade in the main hand, dagger in the off hand. The dagger does not strike in a full attack; it parries (band as above). After a parry, the next main-hand strike gains +10.
    - `buckler_play` (Buckler Play, Lv6): one-handed weapon and a buckler. The block band uses the better of Shields and the weapon skill. The bash itself is `boss_strike` (M3-30), not part of the stance.
    - `phalanx` (Phalanx, Lv8): spear and shield. Opportunity attacks cost 2 AP, and an adjacent ally gains the shield's cover [A].
    - `long_guard` (Long Guard, Lv10): a two-handed weapon. `full_weight` damage doubles and `carry_through` also fires on a critical strike; the evasion band shrinks by 10.
    - `bare_knuckle` (Bare Knuckle, Lv6): both hands empty. +2 unarmed damage and one extra unarmed strike in a full attack at -20.
    - `forward_guard` and `low_guard` (any loadout, Lv4): +10 to attack and -10 evasion band, or the reverse.
