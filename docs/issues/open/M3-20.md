#### M3-20 Perks raise the number of active summons: one, two at about level 14, three at 28
- Status: doing
- Priority: medium
- Needs: M3-10
- Do: Owner request, 2026-11-14: summons need a cap, raised only by perks. Without a perk a caster has one active summon; a perk at about level 14 allows a second, and a perk at about level 28 a third, the second needing the first [A]. A new summon past the cap dismisses the oldest one [A], as a new concentration spell ends the old (docs/core/casting.md). The cap is a number on a modifier target, such as `summon_limit` in `modifier_targets`, so the perks are ordinary modifiers. The cap counts summoned creatures. Raise Horde and Conjured Legion become minion spells with a rolled count (M3-25), and minions do not count toward it (owner decision, 2026-11-14). The summoner archetypes (M3-21 to M3-23) build on this cap.
- Keys: the `summon_limit` target; two summon-limit perks in `features/core.json`
