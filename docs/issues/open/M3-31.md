#### M3-31 Trip and disarm: maneuvers a perk unlocks, and rider perks that put one on every attack
- Status: todo
- Priority: medium
- Needs: M3-24, M3-28
- Modified: 2026-11-18
- Do: Owner request, 2026-11-18: every maneuver is unlocked by a perk (M3-24 makes this the rule). This issue adds the next maneuvers and a second kind of perk that carries a maneuver's effect onto normal attacks.
  - **Unlock perks.** Each new maneuver has one perk that lists it in `unlocks`; holding it lets you attempt the maneuver. Starting rows: **trip** (on a hit, the target makes a check or falls `prone`; the `knockdown` effect gains the `maneuver` channel) and **disarm** (on a hit, the target makes a check or drops its weapon, the same `disarm` event the arms called shot uses). Their AP, attack, and the check's attribute are [A] and set in the spec step. Shove and the others stay for later rows.
  - **Rider perks.** A second perk per maneuver, which needs its unlock perk, puts that maneuver's effect on the actor's normal attacks: every hit of a plain attack may trip, or may disarm. Whether the rider costs the attack anything (a smaller chance, a weaker check) is [A].
  - **Riders block each other.** An actor holds at most one rider. Buying a rider perk replaces the rider held before, and the perk spent on it stays spent: no refund, as summon nature works today (docs/modules/monsters.md#summoned-creatures). So a character who goes for tripping attacks gives up disarming attacks unless they pay again.
  - **Perk respec.** A GM may allow, and a game may sell, a perk respec: the actor takes back perks and buys again with the same perk points. A respec touches only perks; attributes, tag skills, spent skill points, and anything rolled stay as they are. Written once in docs/core/progression.md, so the rider rule and summon nature share it.
  - **One shape for "latest bought replaces".** Today summon power and nature say it in prose. The spec step decides a data field for it (for example a group key on the feature, where a newly bought perk replaces the held perk of the same group) [A], and applies it to the riders and, if it fits, to summon power and nature.
  - **Monsters use the same perks** (owner 2026-11-18). M3-24 lets a monster row or template list `maneuvers` directly, with no perk. Here a monster may also hold the unlock perks and the rider perks in its `perks` (M3-15), as a player does: its usable maneuvers are the ones it lists plus the ones its perks unlock, and a rider perk puts its effect on the monster's plain attacks. The one-rider rule holds for monsters too [A]. `check_monster_perks` and the monster sheet (`test_bestiary.py`, and the Go `monster_sheet`, which does not show maneuvers yet) follow. A vector or test: a monster with a rider perk trips on a plain attack.
  - **Names are original** (owner 2026-11-16): no feat or perk name from another game.
