#### M3-22 Commander summoner perks: the caster spends turns to drive their summons

- Do: Owner request, 2026-11-14: the second summoner archetype (M3-21 to M3-23). The commander's summons are the main damage dealers, and the caster spends their own turn to drive them. Add a perk ladder with two ways to do it. A command is an ability that costs the caster AP (or their whole turn) and gives an active summon AP or another bonus, such as attack, evasion, or damage, for its next turn [A]. A held command is a concentration spell: while the caster concentrates on it, every active summon gains a smaller bonus, and the one-held-spell rule of docs/core/casting.md applies, so a commander cannot also hold Summon Elemental's concentration unless the spec allows it [A]. Higher rungs make a command cheaper, reach more summons at once, or give more AP [A]. AP given to a summon follows the one rule set for turn-based and real-time play (M3-11), so in real time it reads as the same rate change [A]. The summons gain their bonus through the session step that applies a modifier to another actor (M3-14). Write how a player and a GM run it in `docs/guide/`.
