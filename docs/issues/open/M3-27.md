#### M3-27 One attack roll: defense is a band, a shield is DR, block and dodge are declared stances
- Status: todo
- Priority: medium
- Needs: M5-56
- Modified: 2026-11-16
- Do: Owner request, 2026-11-16: make the per-hit maths lighter while keeping block and dodge as table actions. Today every hit takes two d100 rolls (the attack, then the defender's evade or block reaction with a margin comparison) and two subtractions (shield DR on a block, then armor DR) (docs/core/combat.md steps 1, 2, 5, 6). Replace that with one roll and one subtraction. This reverses two owner decisions of 2026-11-04 (the defender's reaction roll, and dropping the passive penalty on the attacker); the decision record quotes both and says why.
  - **Passive, always on.** A carried shield's `shield_dr` joins the worn armor DR against types with `armor_dr_applies`, so step 6 subtracts one total and step 5's shield subtraction goes. Shield Training (`add shield_dr 2`) stays as it is. The defender has an `evasion_rate` derived from the Evasion skill, e.g. `floor(evasion / 2)` as a formula tree [A]; armor and encumbrance penalties land on it. The tree must let skill points move it, since the dropped `10 + evasion * 0.15 + AGI * 2` failed on that.
  - **One attack roll, read in bands** (resolution.md unchanged): `roll <= target - defense` is a hit, `roll <= target` with the roll inside the defense band is `evaded` (or `blocked`), `roll > target` is a miss. A crit window sits at the bottom, so a crit always hits [A]. A `single` attack in the evaded band does no damage; an `area` attack does half. `instant` takes no band; its defense stays the save (casting.md).
  - **Dodge (owner pick, option a).** The `dodge` action (3 AP, not refunded) is a stance until the actor's next turn: the evasion rate doubles [A]. An `area` attack in the evaded band still does half damage, dodging or not (owner, 2026-11-16): the stance widens the band, it does not remove area damage. Sidestep (`add dodge -1`) is unchanged. The video game's dodge input is this stance for the next incoming attack, with the same numbers.
  - **Block.** The `block` action (2 AP, reserved, refunded if unused, as today) is a stance that adds a block band of `block_rate = floor(shields / 2)` [A] below the evaded band; a roll in it is `blocked` and does no damage. Block bands apply only to types with `armor_dr_applies`; Spell Ward (`grant block_magic`) extends it to `single` and `area` magic at half width [A].
  - **Perks keep their hooks through the stances.** Riposte works only while dodging: a roll in the lowest numbers of the evaded band, as wide as the defender's crit window [A], sets `counter`. Bulwark works only while blocking: the same window of the block band staggers the attacker (they lose 2 AP on their next turn) [A], since a block already does no damage. Armored Evasion stays `add evasion 10`.
  - **Damage order** after the roll: damage dice, attribute modifier, called-shot multiplier, crit, area halving, one DR subtraction (armor plus shield), resist, riders.
