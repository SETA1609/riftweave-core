#### M3-19 Commanding Summons has two ranks: half, then the full CHA modifier
- Status: review
- Priority: low
- Needs: M3-13
- Do: Owner request, 2026-11-14: the summoning CHA bonus of M3-13 grows in two steps. Rank 1 of a Commanding Summons perk adds half the caster's CHA modifier, `(CHA − 5) / 2`, to the chosen group; rank 2 adds the full modifier, `CHA − 5`. The half rounds down, and both ranks stay floored at 0, as M3-13 is [A]. At CHA 8 that is +1, then +3; at CHA 10, +2, then +5. The perk's `ranks` becomes 2, with a level or a CHA prerequisite on rank 2 [A]. The summon bonus row (`summons/bonuses.json`) states the fraction per rank, so the rule stays data and is not a special case in the merge [A].
- Keys: `ranks: 2` on the Commanding Summons perks; a per-rank fraction on `summon_bonuses`
- Files: ~7 (docs/modules/magic.md, docs/guide/quick-start.md, ruleset/schemas/summon_bonus.schema.json, ruleset/data/summons/bonuses.json, ruleset/data/features/core.json, ruleset/scripts/test_spells.py, ruleset/tests/vectors/)
- See: docs/issues/open/M3-13.md, docs/modules/attributes.md#the-attribute-modifier
- Done when: a vector shows a summon from a CHA 8 caster gaining +1 on the chosen group at rank 1 and +3 at rank 2, and a CHA 10 caster +2 and +5; CHA 5 or below gives 0 at both ranks; `validate.py` and the tests pass.
