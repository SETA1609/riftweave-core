#### M3-25 Raise Horde and Conjured Legion call minions, not summons
- Status: todo
- Priority: medium
- Needs: M3-10, M3-26
- Do: Owner decision, 2026-11-14: Raise Horde (level 22, 3 undead) and Conjured Legion (level 28, 3 elementals) are dropped as summons. They call minions, or the two spells are dropped if minions do not hold up in the spec [A]. Minions are scapegoats: many weak bodies that soak attacks and spread a foe's actions, not creatures that grow. A minion comes from a monster row at the caster's level, with a `minion` template of maluses on top: lower attributes and skills, and lower HP, so ordinary attacks drop it quickly [A]. Set the sizes in the spec against the balance bands [U]. Summon spells are custom rows that only a GM or dev adds, so they may leave the shared spell shape: a summon spell writes a `summon` block in place of its `core:effect/summon` entry (owner decision, 2026-11-14). `appliedEffect` does not change, so potions and enchantments are untouched. The block has two parts. The base part, `group` (a `core:summon/<key>` ref) and `duration`, is all a single summon needs, and every existing summon spell moves to it. The minion part, `count` and `minion`, is used only by minion spells. `count` is a number or a dice expression, such as `1d6`, rolled when the spell is cast [A]. A count from the cast's margin (`1 + ⌊margin / 20⌋`, capped) is a later variant, not built here [A]. Mana is set for the mean count, so the casting cost rule does not change [A]. Horde and Legion are composed spells (M3-26): Conjured Legion is `base: core:spell/summon_elemental` with overrides for its level, `summon.count: "1d6"`, and `summon.minion: true`, and Raise Horde builds on `raise_thrall` the same way, so they inherit the base spell's qualities and add only their extras. Minions do not count toward the summon cap (M3-20) and get no summoning bonuses: no CHA bonus (M3-13, M3-19), no power or nature template (M3-10), and none of the summoner archetype perks (M3-21 to M3-23). Minions stay in core for now (owner, 2026-11-14). Later, minions and multiple summons move to an optional module, leaving only single summons in core. So the spec keeps the minion part in its own section, and no core rule depends on it: the move is then the minion part of the schema, the `minion` template, and the two composed spells going to the module.
