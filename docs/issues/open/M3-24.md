#### M3-24 Combat maneuvers: actions that apply effects for AP and stamina, not mana
- Status: todo
- Priority: medium
- Needs: M3-10
- Do: Owner request, 2026-11-14: fighters need actions that reference effects the way spells do, but cost no mana or little: power attack, quick attack, and other combat maneuvers (trip, disarm, shove, cleave, and so on [A]). Add a `maneuvers` collection (`core:maneuver/<key>`; not `abilities`, which holds the attributes [A]). A maneuver names the action it modifies (`attack`, like `aimed` with `adds_to`), its extra AP at a table, which is stamina in real time at `stamina_per_ap` (docs/core/actions.md), an optional small mana cost [A], the skills or weapon types it needs, and its effects from the shared pool (`appliedEffect`), with a new `maneuver` channel checked by `CHANNEL_REQUIREMENTS`. Starting rows: a power attack (more AP, more damage, a heavy weapon's surcharge, which `brutal_charge` already refers to) and a quick attack (fewer AP, less damage) [A]. Perks unlock maneuvers through `unlocks` (M3-10); decide whether the passive `power_attack` perk becomes the unlock for the power attack maneuver or stays a passive bonus [A]. Monsters use the same rows: a monster row or template lists `maneuvers`, so a troll can power attack without a spell-like ability written for it [A]. The combat pipeline resolves a maneuver as its base action plus its effects, in Python and Go.
