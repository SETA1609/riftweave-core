- Do: Owner request, 2026-11-19: in combat, a player sees which actions their character has unlocked and what each costs, as a menu. No kernel function answers this today: costs are spread over `ruleset/data/actions/core.json`, the loadout rows of `attack_sequences/core.json`, perk modifiers on an action's key, maneuvers a perk unlocks, stances, spells, and summon orders, and only `actions` sums the cost of a list the caller already chose [V 2026-11-19, `backend/contract/actions.json`].
  - **The spec:** a section `## The action menu` in `docs/core/actions.md`. A new kernel function `action_menu` takes the actor (attributes, perks, known spells, conditions, the loadout), the AP left, the current stamina and mana, and the active summons, and lists every action the actor holds.
  - **What it lists:** the 14 rows of `actions/core.json`; the attack, jab, full attack, and circle cut at their loadout row's cost; maneuvers the actor's perks unlock (GD_S05_04), with their AP and attack penalty; stances the actor's perks give, with the cost of taking one now (GD_S05_02); each known spell at the cast AP and its mana cost; and `command` when a perk gives orders (GD_S06_03). The rows story S19 adds (`draw`, `sheathe`, `swap`, `ready`) join the list as they merge, and once `GD_S19_05_ap-bank-cap-and-guard` lands, the menu also shows the AP the actor will hold, and the guard it gives, if they end the turn now.
  - **Each entry:** `key`, `kind` (`action`, `attack`, `maneuver`, `stance`, `spell`, `order`), `ap`, `stamina`, `mana` (when it has one), and `available`. A perk's `add` on the key changes the cost as in `actions`, never below 0, so Sidestep shows dodge at 2.
  - **Why not:** an entry the actor holds but cannot take now stays in the list with `available: false` and a `why` from a fixed set: `ap` (not enough AP left; a full attack needs the attack's AP + 1), `mana`, `stamina`, `loadout` (the hands do not fit, such as circle cut without a two-handed weapon), `condition` (`stand_up` only while prone), and `no_summon` (an order with no summon out).
  - **Not unlocked means not listed** [A]: an action no perk of the actor gives (Circle Cut without the perk, a maneuver without its unlock perk) is left out, so the menu is the character's own. Whether locked entries should show with what unlocks them is the open question below.
  - **Order:** by `kind`, then the `actions/core.json` order, then by key [A].
  - The Python reference goes in `kernel_reference.py`, with vectors in `ruleset/tests/vectors/action_menu.json`.
