#### M3-15 Monsters and NPCs take perks
- Status: review
- Priority: medium
- Needs: M3-10
- Do: Owner request, 2026-11-12: monsters, mobs, and NPCs can hold perks (feats) to empower them, beside the spell and ability lists M3-10 gives them. Players and monsters are one actor shape (M5-28), so a monster takes perks from the same `features` table. Monster rows and monster templates gain `perks`: `core:feature/<key>` refs with an optional rank, capped by the perk's `ranks`. A template's perks add to the row's, as its spells do, so `dire` can grant Toughness and `elder` Second Wind [A]. The perk's modifiers reach the monster's sheet through the same pipeline and origin stacking as a player's. Prerequisites are not checked on a monster: an author or GM places the perk on purpose [A]. Some perks mean nothing on a monster (barter, crafting, social); `validate.py` warns, not fails, when a monster or template names a perk of the `social` or `utility` category [A]. The monster sheet (`monster_sheet` in Python and Go) applies its perks' modifiers to its derived stats and skills. Add perks to a few rows as examples (a bandit captain, a troll, the dire and elder templates).
- Keys: `perks` on monster rows and monster templates
- Files: ~10 (docs/modules/monsters.md, docs/core/actor-sheet.md, ruleset/schemas/monster.schema.json, ruleset/schemas/monster_template.schema.json, ruleset/data/monsters/core.json, ruleset/data/monsters/templates.json, ruleset/scripts/validate.py, ruleset/scripts/kernel_reference.py, ruleset/tests/vectors/monster_sheet.json, backend/internal/derived/)
