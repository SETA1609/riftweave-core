#### M3-12 An attribute modifier adds to attack damage
- Status: done
- Merged: #119, 2026-11-11
- Priority: high
- Needs: none
- Do: An attribute's modifier is `attribute − 5`: +5 at 10, 0 at 5, −4 at 1. Define it once as data, for every actor. Weapon damage is the weapon's dice plus the modifier of the attribute the attack uses. A melee weapon uses `str`, and a weapon with the `finesse` property uses `agi`. A ranged weapon depends on how quick or heavy it is, and no existing property says that (a pistol and an arquebus both have `loading`), so each ranged weapon names its attribute in a new `ability` field. Slow weapons use `per`: `arquebus`, `crossbow`, `light_crossbow`, `longbow`, and `blunderbuss` [A]. Quick weapons use `agi`: `flintlock_pistol`, `hand_crossbow`, a repeating crossbow, `shortbow`, `composite_bow` (a recurve), and `bow`, `improvised_bow`, `sling`, and `throwing_knife` [A]. Heavy thrown weapons use `str`: `javelin`, `throwing_axe`, a throwing hammer, and improvised throws such as a rock. Add the three weapons that do not exist yet: `repeating_crossbow` (1d6), `throwing_hammer` (1d6), and `thrown_rock` (1d4, no cost) [A]. Other rules take the same modifier when they name an attribute, and what the action does decides which one. A player's starting attributes keep their cap of 10 at character creation; racial and perk bonuses can pass it. In the combat pipeline the modifier adds at the damage step, before the called-shot multiplier and the crit [A], and damage stops at zero as every step does. Check the result against the balance bands and list any weapon or armor change in the PR [U].
