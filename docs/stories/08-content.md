## M6 Content parity

**Goal:** after `v1.0.0`, core content reaches the size of the D&D SRD 5.2, and the rules are easy to run at a table. Content counts as equal where Riftweave builds things from parts: spells from the effect pool, monster variants from templates, ordinary magic items from base, material, and enchantment. So the targets are about 220 perks, about 200 written spells over about 150 effects, about 150 base monsters, and about 100 named items, plus encounter budgets, travel, pre-built characters, and a tabletop playtest. The table module (POST-10 to POST-12) and the printouts (APP-11) serve the same goal from their own stories. Plan: `docs/decisions/M6-01.md`.

**Spec:** `docs/core/` for kernel rules, `docs/modules/` for module rules.

**Done when:** the content targets above are met and pass the balance checks; a referee can budget an encounter and a day of travel from data; six pre-built characters exist; three tabletop sessions with new players are logged (M6-13).
