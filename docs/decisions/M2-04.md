# M2-04 Races and backgrounds emit modifiers only

Date: 2026-11-06

- **Decision:** a race reaches the actor only as contract modifiers. `abilityModifiers` is the
  compact form of `add` (source `race:<key>`, origin `innate`), each racial trait carries its own
  `modifiers` (source `feature:<key>`, origin `innate`), and a background's `skill_bonuses` is the
  compact form of `add` (source `background:<key>`, origin `trained`). A subrace emits its
  parent's modifiers and traits, then its own; a kin emits only its own; a template is never
  played. `ruleset/scripts/ancestry_reference.py`, `test_ancestry.py` (lineage schema rules for
  all five roles, and the emitted modifiers). Spec: `docs/modules/race.md#5a-what-a-race-emits`,
  `docs/modules/backgrounds.md`. **[V 2026-11-06]**
- **Decision:** the 18 racial traits had no structured effect, so their rules lived only in
  descriptions. Each now has modifiers; senses and proficiencies are `grant` flags, numbers are
  `add` on a skill or a named target (`darkvision_range`, `tag_skills`, `hp_per_level`), and
  catman claws are a `natural_weapon` (1d6 slashing), a new optional feature field. **[V 2026-11-06]**
- **Why:** the issue's rule is that no mechanical statement exists only in a description.
- **Open:** situational traits (shadowmeld, pack tactics, keen smell) are `add` on situational
  targets the caller reads when the situation holds; the modifier has no condition field
  **[A]**. Elf keen senses adds to `investigation`, which also feeds `deduce`, not only `search`
  **[A]**. Dwarven resilience is 25 % on a percent target; the kernel `resist` is flat, so no
  runner reads it yet **[U]**. No runner reads `tag_skills`, `creation_perks`, or
  `hp_per_level` yet (character creation, M2-03) **[U]**.
