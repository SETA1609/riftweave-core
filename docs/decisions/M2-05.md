# M2-05 Equipment to the content floor

Date: 2026-11-06

- **Decision:** 33 weapons (13 new), 13 accessories (10 new), 22 consumables (15 new), and 18
  gear rows (8 tools, 10 adventuring gear) in a new `equipment/gear.json`, refs
  `core:gear/<key>`. `ruleset/scripts/test_equipment.py` checks the floor.
  **[V 2026-11-06, `python -m unittest ruleset/scripts/test_equipment.py`]**
- **Decision:** "across tiers" is read as price tiers: under 5 gp, under 25 gp, from 25 gp.
  Each weapon skill has three weapons in at least two of them. `unarmed`, `guns`, and
  `crossbows` had none or one; `throwing_weapons` had only the sling. **[A]**
- **Decision:** a tool emits `add` on the skill it helps, source `item:<key>`, origin
  `core:origin/equipment`, in a new optional `gear` block (`modifiers`, `uses`) on the
  equipment schema. The Python loot reference, `validate.py`, and the Go loader know the
  `gear` category, so loot rows may name gear. **[V 2026-11-06]**
- **Decision:** the unused `crossbow` base had no `skill`; it now rolls `crossbows` and is a
  row. **[V 2026-11-06]**
- **Decision:** the throwing knife and brass knuckles roll 1d4, below the weapon dice band;
  they are accepted findings in `balance/core.json`, as the dagger and the sling are.
  **[V 2026-11-06, `balance_report.py --gate`]**
- **Decision:** a pool effect with no `duration` is instant; with one, `magnitude` is per
  second. Instant healing was too strong: Minor Healing restored 50 HP of a level-1
  character's 59, and the Potion of Healing out-healed an average character's whole pool up
  to level 10. Minor, normal, and greater healing are now 20, 40, and 80; mana 15; stamina 30.
  Food is always over time (stew 1 a second for 60 s, rations 1 for 20 s, bread and cheese 1
  HP for 10 s), and Poison of Weakness is 2 a second for 6 s (12, was 10). **[V 2026-11-07,
  owner decision; `test_equipment.py` checks food durations]**
- **Decision:** `weapons.md` has a reference for weapon dice: one dice ladder, a dice range
  per handling (light, one-handed, two-handed, heavy two-handed) for medium weapons, and size as
  a field of the weapon, not the wielder: `size` `undersized` (−1 rung), `normal` (the
  default when omitted), `oversized` (+1). The ladder is `combat/dice_ladder.json`; rows
  keep base dice and the balance report checks the sized dice. No row is
  sized: a sized weapon is composed where it is used, as a material is. Size is not an
  enchantment, so it uses no slot and a dispel leaves it. Undersized is easier to use (+10
  attack), oversized harder (−10); the values are **[A]**. **[V 2026-11-07, owner decision]**
- **Decision:** rows are only what the rules cannot build: each distinct base weapon, and
  named items that differ on purpose. Generic variants (a material, a size) are composed
  where they are used, so the undersized shortbow and oversized longbow rows are gone and
  the size test builds them from the bases. Loot rows get `size` in M4-07.
  **[V 2026-11-07, owner decision]**
- **Decision:** `duration` is seconds, an integer from 1, and 1 (the default when omitted)
  is instant. Magnitude is always per second; one effect serves instant and over time, with
  no split into two effects. On a worn item a stat effect holds while equipped and takes no
  duration; an on-hit effect uses its duration per hit. Two crafted enchantments had
  `duration: 0`: the frost hit is now 1, the worn magic resist has none.
  **[V 2026-11-07, owner decision]**
- **Decision:** `valid_materials` is now `default_materials`: the materials a base is found
  and sold in, not a limit. Any material may be crafted onto any base, as in Elin (a granite
  bow), and `validate.py` no longer rejects a crafted item outside the list. Odd combinations
  are player-crafted only. **[V 2026-11-07, owner decision]**
- **Decision:** every distance is in units, one grid square each; for tabletop play a
  square is 1.5 m. Feet became units by fives (a human walks 6, the longbow reaches 30 /
  120); metres by 1.5 (Fire Burst radius 4, the monster burst radius 3, darkvision 12 and
  24, the safe fall 4, the rope 10). The spell area cost is +0.15 per unit of radius, the
  same as +0.1 per metre. Nothing in the Python reference or Go reads a distance.
  **[V 2026-11-07, owner decision; `docs/core/contract.md#distance`]** Every row stays medium. **[A]**
- **Why:** accessories and consumables were far below playable, and four weapon skills had
  almost nothing to wield.
- **Open:** guns in a fantasy SRD are flintlocks and a matchlock; the `loading` property has
  no rule yet **[U]**. Accessory effects use the `resist` parameters `frost` and `physical`,
  which no damage pipeline step names yet **[U]**. A tool's modifiers apply only to the check
  it is used for; nothing enforces that **[A]**. The tick rule, the duration unit (seconds in
  `schema.json`, turns in `contract.md`), food refused in combat, and a new dish replacing the
  last one's effects are M2-07 **[U]**. Stamina has no formula, so the stamina numbers are
  provisional **[U]**. The combat pipeline takes the damage roll as input, so the caller rolls the
  sized dice; no vector covers it **[A]**. The size attack bonus is prose and data; no combat
  vector covers it **[A]**. Loot does not read `default_materials`: a loot row's
  `material` lands on every weapon and armor it yields (the stone golem's steel hoard can
  drop a steel bow). Falling back to the item's first default material is M4-07 **[U]**.
  `ruleset/scripts/combat_reference.py` still reads `defaultDuration` above 0 as timed, so it
  calls the five on-hit effects (now 1) timed; the script already fails before that on an
  unknown weapon ref, and nothing runs it **[U]**.
