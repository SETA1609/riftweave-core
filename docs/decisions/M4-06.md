# M4-06 Looter shift: Luck and perks on loot rolls

Date: 2026-11-06

- **Decision (owner):** the looter's shift is the value of target `loot_roll`, starting at 0,
  after the looter's modifiers and the kernel stacking rules. Luck emits
  `add loot_roll ((LCK - 5) * 2)` (`ruleset/data/formulas/loot_luck.json`, source `attr:lck`,
  origin innate), and the Scavenger perk gives +10 per rank. The shift adds to every table roll
  (kept between 1 and 100) and to every `chance` (kept between 1 and 99), for a monster and for a
  table rolled on its own. Spec: `docs/core/loot.md#looter`. 12 new vectors. **[V 2026-11-06]**
- **Why:** loot read only the monster's rows, so who looted did not matter. Luck already widens
  the crit window by emitting a modifier ([resolution.md](../core/resolution.md)); loot uses the
  same pattern, so a perk, a potion, or a curse needs no loot rule of its own.
- **Open:** LCK 5 is neutral so the budget, checked at a shift of 0, is the average looter's
  **[A]**. The chance clamp at 99 keeps a drop from ever being certain **[A]**. Perk `effects`
  are not turned into modifiers by any runner yet: the caller passes the Scavenger bonus as an
  `add loot_roll` modifier **[U]**.
- **Risk:** a lucky looter beats the treasure budget on average, by design; a balance pass
  (M4-05) should check the top of the curve, LCK 10 with two Scavenger ranks (+30).
