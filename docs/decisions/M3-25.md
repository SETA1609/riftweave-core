# M3-25 Raise Horde and Conjured Legion call minions, not summons

Date: 2026-11-16
Modified: 2026-11-16

- **Decision:** a summon spell writes a `summon` block (`group`, `duration`) in place of its
  `core:effect/summon` entry, and a minion spell adds `minion: true` and `count`. Raise Horde and
  Conjured Legion are composed spells on Raise Thrall and Summon Elemental with `count: "1d3+1"`.
  A cast adds that many group refs to `caster.minions`, outside the summon limit; a minion is its
  row at the caster's level with the `minion` template and no summoning bonus. Minions held up in
  the spec, so neither spell is dropped. **[V 2026-11-16, `validate.py`, the unit tests, and
  `go test ./...`, with eight casting vectors and two minion tests]**
- **Difference from the issue: the count is `1d3+1`, not `1d6`.** The issue gave `1d6` as an
  example and set mana for the mean. A mean of 3.5 prices the spells at 126 mana, over a level 22
  caster's pool of 108 (`test_a_spell_is_affordable_at_its_level`), so Raise Horde could not be
  cast at its own level. `1d3+1` (two to four, mean 3) keeps the owner's original three creatures
  on average and the 108 mana the spells cost before. The vector and spec commits were corrected
  before the data step; no vector changed after it.
- **How a dice count is rolled:** as every die is an input, the caller passes the total as
  `count_roll`; a dice count without it, or with a total its dice cannot make, is an error, checked
  before anything is spent. **[A]**
- **The block is priced as the summon effect it replaces** (group, count or mean count, duration,
  target `ranged`), in `spell_cost`, the integrity gate (a summon block counts as a use of
  `core:effect/summon`), and the Go author check, so the cost rule does not change. **[A]**
- **The minion template:** every attribute −2 (never below 1) and `hit_points_multiplier` 0.25,
  applied after the attribute adds. A level 22 skeleton minion has under half the plain row's HP.
  These are first numbers to set against the balance bands in the M5-50 pacing pass. **[U]**
- **Kept apart for the module move:** the minion rules are their own section in `casting.md`
  (`#minions`) and `monsters.md` (`#minions`); no other rule reads `minion`, `count`, or
  `caster.minions`, so the later module takes the block's minion part, the template, and the two
  spells.
- **Not done:** minions are tracked only as group refs, like summons; their duration and dismissal
  are left to the caller, as for summons. Go has no monster stat generation, so the minion's stats
  are checked in Python (`monster_reference.minion`, `test_spells.py`). The quick-start guide does
  not mention minions yet. The frontend's API types were not regenerated for `count_roll` and
  `minions`.
- **Risk:** breaking for a module spell that names `core:effect/summon` (it now needs a summon
  block to call anything) and for a caller that reads a summon spell's `effects` for its creatures.
  Marked in the changelog.
