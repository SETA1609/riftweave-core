# M2-01 Perk catalogue to the content floor

Date: 2026-11-06

- **Decision:** 76 perks (14 before, 62 new) at five level tiers (1, 6, 12, 18, 24, from
  `prerequisite.level`). Every perk emits contract modifiers, source `feature:<key>`,
  origin `core:origin/trained`; the 14 older perks keep their `effects` prose, now read from
  their new `modifiers`. Spec: `docs/modules/progression.md#what-a-perk-emits`.
  **[V 2026-11-06]**
- **Decision:** a ranked perk's modifier values are per rank; a consumer emits each `add` at
  value times rank, as one modifier, since one source keeps only its strongest
  (`modifier.md`). **[A]**
- **Decision:** "no perk is dominated by another at the same level" is checked as: no two
  perks at one tier emit the same set of targets and ops. `ruleset/scripts/test_perks.py`
  checks it with the count, the eight attribute gates, the combat, magic, and social groups
  (at least eight each), and that every prerequisite perk exists at a lower or equal tier.
  **[V 2026-11-06, `python -m unittest ruleset/scripts/test_perks.py`]**
- **Decision:** perks that change an AP cost emit `add` on the action key (`cast`, `aimed`),
  as `actions.md` asks. `battle_mage` halves the casting armor penalty of M3-01, and
  `unraveler` adds a level to dispel. **[A]**
- **Why:** a character gains a perk at most levels; with 14 perks there was no choice.
- **Open:** most targets are named targets no kernel function reads yet
  (`sneak_attack_damage_percent`, `thrall_limit`, `trap_detect`); they are rules for the
  consumer, as the M2-04 racial traits are **[U]**. Haggler and Silver Tongue both improve
  barter at tier 1; Silver Tongue does more but needs Persuasion 50 and CHA 6, so it is not
  a copy **[A]**. The ten `creation` features still carry only `effects`, no `modifiers`
  **[U]**. The numbers are not balanced against the M4-05 bands **[U]**.
