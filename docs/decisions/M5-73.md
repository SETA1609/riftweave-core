# M5-73 Third normal form: an override lists only what differs

Date: 2026-11-17

- **Decision:** an override object merges field by field into the base's, each field by the same rule, so an override names only what differs. `$extend` still appends to a list, and a new marker, `{"$replace": value}`, replaces a field whole. Python (`_merge_field`, `compose_row`) and Go (`data.MergeOverride` in the item loader) merge the same way. [A in the issue, chosen here]
- **`$replace` keeps nine rows unchanged:** nine derived monsters set a `resist` that, under the old whole-object merge, dropped their base monster's resistances: the venom, storm, and acid elementals and the magma titan (from the fire elemental), the bronze guardian and the iron colossus (stone golem), the elder treant (treant sapling), and the storm giant youth and frost giant (giant). They now say `$replace`, so no merged row changes. **Owner question:** were the dropped resistances intended (a venom elemental without fire resistance seems right; a bronze guardian without the golem's slashing and piercing 3 is less clear)? If not, drop `$replace` on those rows and they will inherit.
- **The copies dropped:** 119 members that equal what the row inherits (after its base and its templates): 30 in equipment (the cost units and phases of accessories, armor, and weapons), 85 in monsters (mostly `phase` and `size`, plus `tags`, `hitPoints`, and sub-fields of `attributes`), and the `phase` of four subraces. [V 2026-11-17]
- **Proof that nothing moved** [V 2026-11-17]: every merged equipment and monster row from `validate.py`'s resolution is byte-identical before and after (a JSON dump of both, compared). The Go ruleset's item prices, dice, skills, DR, default materials, monster levels, and monster loot are identical too, with one difference: an accessory's `Type` now reads `accessory` from its base, as the Python row always did; Go reads `Type` only to find food.
- **The gate** (`docs/core/integrity.md` gate 5): `compose_row` reports each override path equal to the inherited value, and `validate.py` fails it; a subrace field equal to its parent's fails in the race checks. A derived monster's `level` is exempt, because an older rule makes a derived monster state its level.
- **Readers that skipped composition:** `loot_reference.item_field` and `monster_level` and `test_equipment.resolved_weapons` read override fields directly; they now merge as `compose_row` does, and `monster_level` follows a base monster. The Go monster level does the same.
- **Breaking:** a consumer that merges overrides itself must merge objects field by field and understand `$replace`. Module data written for the old rule may now fail the gate where it repeats a base value.
- **Overlap:** M3-24 (open) adds `maneuvers` to the troll's overrides in `monsters/core.json`; the two edits touch different members of that row and should rebase cleanly. M5-74 (stored monster HP) comes next on the same file.
