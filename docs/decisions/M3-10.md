# M3-10 Power and nature templates for summons and monsters

Date: 2026-11-12

- **Decision:** templates have a `slot` (`element`, `power`, `nature`), at most one per slot on a row and its base monsters together, applied element, power, then nature whatever the listed order. A template may add attributes (an END add moves an authored `hitPoints` by the `hp` row's `per_point`), resistances, spells, tags, and loot; `phase` and `damage_type` are required only for an element. Spec: `docs/modules/monsters.md#templates`. **[V 2026-11-12, `test_bestiary.py`]**
- **Decision:** the power ladder is `dire`, then `elder`, then `ancient`: perks `summon_dire`, `summon_elder` (needs `summon_dire`), `summon_ancient` (needs `summon_elder`), each needing Orange Magic (40, 60, 80) and a level (8, 16, 24). The issue listed `dire, ancient, elder`; the ladder puts elder before ancient, as "elder needs dire" asks. The natures are `summon_demonic`, `summon_angelic`, `summon_otherworldly` (level 12, Orange Magic 50), with no prerequisite on each other. Each perk names its template in the new `unlocks` field, typed as a monster template or a material ref so M3-09 can use it. A perk must emit a modifier, so the power perks add Orange Magic +5 and the natures resist fire, radiant, or psychic 5. **[A]**
- **Decision:** a generated monster is a row plus a power and a nature, named `{ monster, power, nature }`, composed by `monster_reference.generate` with no row written for it. A slot the row already fills keeps the row's template, so a dire wolf summoned by an elder caster stays dire. **[A]**
- **Decision:** monster attributes have no maximum (schema and level growth); the minimum of 1 stays. **[V 2026-11-12]**
- **Decision:** every attack names a `weapon`, `core:natural_weapon/<key>` (new collection, ten rows, dice for a medium creature moved tiny −2 to huge +2 on the dice ladder) or a carried `core:weapon/<key>` (its own size only), and rolls it plus `attribute − 5` of `ability` (default `str`). `damage.amount` is gone; `damage.type` is the weapon's unless the attack names one, and an element template sets only an unnamed type. **[V 2026-11-12]** The size steps are **[A]**.
- **Decision:** `growth.damagePerLevel` is 0.25 on every creature type and adds `round(0.25 × Δlevel)` to each attack's `damage.bonus` and each `damage_*` ability's magnitude when a derived or summoned creature is above its row's level. **[V 2026-11-12]** Whether 0.25 keeps rows in their band at every level is **[U]**.
- **Decision:** an attack that is neither natural nor carried is a spell-like ability. The ooze family fights with a touch acid ability (fire and frost through their templates); the flame breath, dragon fire, screech, searing light, draining touch, and force bolt moved to `abilities`. A spell-like ability pays mana by the spell cost formula and the `cast` AP, refuses with too little mana, and costs no mana for a creature with no attacks. No ability names an attribute yet, so none takes the modifier. **[A]**
- **Decision:** the five elementals of the `elemental` summon group have a `slam` that stays bludgeoning, a bolt (7) and a touch (6) of their element; the elemental type gains INT 5 (+0.4 a level). `damage_poison` is new (spell channel, wood, green [A]) for the venom elemental. Other elemental-type rows (wisp, salamander, seraph, void horror, ember drake) keep their own kits. **[A]**
- **Decision:** example rows `dog`, `wolf`, `dire_wolf` (`wolf` + `dire`), `elder_wolf` (`wolf` + `elder`, level 12), `demonic_hound` (`dog` + `demonic`, level 6), and `undead_horse` (undead type, a `hoof`) take their HP from the player formulas at average rolls. All six are above the hits-to-kill band and are accepted by name in `balance/core.json` until the balance pass. **[V 2026-11-12, `balance_report.py`]**
- **Decision:** `dire_wolf` changed: STR 6 → 7, END 4 → 5 (the template), HP 33 → 67 (the player formula); size, level, loot, and abilities are unchanged.
- **Decision:** 23 rows raised an attribute (STR, or AGI where the attack now uses AGI: rat, goblin, spider, mind leech, storm hawk, familiars) so their strongest hit sits in their tier's damage band; the elemental bolts and touches came down to 7 and 6. `balance_report.py` reads an attack as dice mean plus modifier plus bonus. **[V 2026-11-12, `balance_report.py --gate`]**
- **Why:** summons and monsters needed power and nature without a row per combination, attacks needed dice and attributes like a player's, and the 1–10 attribute cap stopped high-level monsters from growing.
- **Open:** the monster attack and ability use are Python reference functions (`monster_reference.py`) tested in `test_bestiary.py`; they have no contract command or Go code yet, since Go reads only monster levels and loot. Whether a game locks nature as a one-time choice. The `skill.schema.json` seed text now names `skill_start`.
- **Risk:** the example rows' player-formula HP is two to three times the authored HP of the other rows, so a fight against them runs long until the balance pass.
