# M5-68 File the melee and defense perk issues

Date: 2026-11-17

- **Decision:** the melee and defense perks become four issues: M3-28, M3-29, M3-30, and M5-67. They run as one chain (M5-67, M3-28, M3-29, M3-30) because each edits `ruleset/data/features/core.json`. M5-47 (the melee builds, already in review) does not wait for them: it merges with the perks of 2026-11-16, and M3-30 moves its builds onto the new ladders. Owner request, 2026-11-16 and 2026-11-17. **[V 2026-11-17, `roadmap-order show`]**
- **Owner answers, 2026-11-16:**
  - Two-weapon fighting has off-hand penalties. Perks add strikes to a full attack, which spends all AP on one target, and the strikes resolve one after the other.
  - Stances are toggles.
  - How attacks stack is a schema or a table (`attack_sequences`).
  - `cleave` needs `power_attack`, and `power_attack` opens to every melee weapon skill.
  - Perk names are original: no feat or perk name from another game (M5-67). The spear-and-shield stance is `phalanx`.
- **Owner answers, 2026-11-17:**
  - One `attack` strikes with the main hand and the off-hand weapon. Its AP cost depends on the loadout: one weapon, two-handed, or two weapons and which ones.
  - A jab strikes with the off-hand weapon alone, at that weapon's cost.
  - Spell Ward no longer stops a spell. When a blockable spell lands in the block band, it resists `END x 2 x tier` percent, capped at 50%; a spell that is not blocked deals full damage. A table lists the percentages so a player and a GM do no arithmetic.
  - Texts keep blockable spells (`single`, `area`) apart from instant spells, whose defense is the save.
  - Shield Training has three tiers.
  - No-armor perks improve evasion.
  - Heavier armor gives larger casting penalties, which perks reduce.
  - Covering an adjacent ally is a perk (`guardian`), not a tower shield property.
  - Shield bash is a ladder.
  - Tiers sit 3 to 6 levels apart: no gate, then about Lv6, 12, 18.
  - `acrobat` is dropped from core: its stamina discount is worth less than 1 AP at a table, so it belongs to a real-time engine.
- **Chosen by the agent, owner left it open:**
  - Shield Training gives +2, +3, and +4, so tier 1 stays today's +2.
  - The buckler bash moves from `buckler_play` to `boss_strike`.
  - `battle_mage` becomes `mailed_casting`.
  - A two-handed attack stays 3 AP, since an AGI 3 character has only 3 AP. [A]
- **Open:** every AP cost, penalty, and band width in M3-28 to M3-30 is [A] until the M5-18 playtest driver reruns builds of the same level.
- **Risk:** the renames in M5-67, M3-28, M3-29, and M3-30 break consumers that store perk keys, such as saved sessions and backups (APP-16).
