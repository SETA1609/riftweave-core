# GD_S05_04_trip-and-disarm Trip and disarm: maneuvers a perk unlocks, and rider perks that put one on every attack

Date: 2026-11-19

- **Decision:** four maneuver rows, a melee line and a ranged line: `trip` and `disarm` (melee), `tripping_shot` and `disarming_shot` (ranged). Each is unlocked by its own perk (Low Sweep, Bind and Wrench, Low Aim, Knuckle Shot), and each has one rider perk that needs the unlock perk (Hooking Blows, Wrenching Blows, Hamstring Shots, Stripping Shots). The maneuver schema gains `save_bonus` and `events` (`disarm`, the same event the arms called shot names). Trip's effect is `knockdown` (5 s, one turn), which gains the `maneuver` channel. [A]
- **Numbers:** Trip and Tripping Shot are the attack's AP, -10, x0.5 damage; Disarm and Disarming Shot are the attack's AP, -15 (as the arms called shot), x0.5. All four have `save_bonus` 20. Melee unlock perks: AGI 5 or STR 5 and a melee skill (Blades, Blunt, Piercing, or Unarmed) 30. Ranged unlock perks: PER 5 and a ranged skill (Bows, Crossbows, Guns, or Throwing Weapons) 30. Rider perks: level 8, the unlock perk, and AGI 6, STR 6, or PER 6. [A]
- **The maneuver attempt (owner answers 1 and 3):** the attack resolves as a normal attack. On a hit that is not evaded, a maneuver with `save_bonus` or a rider makes an attempt, a save the defender makes against the attacker's weapon attribute. The attribute is the attack's `damage.ability`, so it follows the weapon (AGI for a blade, STR for a mace, PER for a bow), not a fixed AGI or STR. DC = attribute x `maneuver_save_multiplier` (10, `combat/pipeline.json`) + the maneuver's `save_bonus` (a rider adds none); the defender's d100 save target is max(100 - DC, 0). The result reports `save: {attribute, dc, target}`; the caller rolls the save (answer 2) and applies the effects and events on a failure. An attempt without `damage.ability` is an error. The "mods" are the attacker's modified attributes, which the caller passes; no perk or situation modifies the DC or the save yet. [A]
- **The clear margin is gone (answer 3):** the first draft let a rider ride only on a plain hit that cleared the bands by 20 (`rider_clear_by`) or a crit. With a save, that rule did the same job twice, so it is removed: every hit makes the attempt. The trade-off between a maneuver and a rider is now the +20 on the DC: a maneuver pays -10 or -15 on the attack and half the damage for it, a rider pays nothing and lands less often. [A]
- **Combat input:** `combat` takes `attacker.perks` (refs) and finds the rider among them itself. A rider rides when its maneuver fits the attack, so a melee rider stays off a ranged shot. With a `maneuver` on the attack, only that maneuver applies. Two riders that fit the same attack are an error ("two rider perks fit this attack: ..."). The result reports `rider`, `effects`, `events`, and `save`. Heavy Swing and Quick Strike have no `save_bonus` and land their effects on any hit, as before. [A]
- **Riders in an attack sequence** (review, 2026-11-19): `attack_sequence` passes `attacker.perks` to every strike, so a rider rides each strike it fits, one attempt per strike, and the strike reports `rider`, `effects`, `events`, and `save` as `combat` does; left out on a strike no rider rides. Without this, no rider rode an attack, a jab, or a full attack. [V 2026-11-19]
- **Attack sequences:** every strike of an attack, jab, or full attack reads `attacker.perks` for a rider, as `combat` does. A Boss Strike shield bash (GD_S05_03) carries no rider, because a shield has no weapon attribute for the attempt. [A]
- **Exclusions (answer 9):** a feature `excludes` list. In each range the trip line and the disarm line exclude each other, both perks of each. `take_perk` (Python and Go) refuses a perk that a held perk excludes or that excludes a held perk, with `unmet` `{needs: not_excluded, key}`, sorted by key. `validate.py` checks each exclusion is listed both ways and that no monster or template holds an excluded pair; `monster_sheet` fails one too. The melee lines do not exclude the ranged lines. [A]
- **No replace groups (answer 7):** the `group` field and the `replaced` result are removed (`take_perk`, the session writeback, `build_reference.py`). All perks stay in the list; a higher rank overrides its lower rank (`docs/core/progression.md#ranks-override`). [A]
- **Summon power and nature (answer 8):** `monster_reference.held_templates` now applies the highest power rung held by ladder depth (dire, elder, ancient), whatever the buy order, instead of the latest bought; each rung's modifiers still count. The three natures exclude each other, so a caster holds one, and two natures are an error. [A]
- **Monsters:** a monster's maneuvers are the ones it lists plus the ones its perks unlock; `monster_sheet` (Python and Go) returns `maneuvers` and `riders` (a sorted list, since a melee and a ranged rider may be held together). The wolf holds Low Sweep and Hooking Blows. Perk level gates do not apply to monster rows. Other monsters get maneuver perks later (answer 10).
- **Respec (answer 11):** `docs/core/progression.md#perk-respec`: only perks are given back and rebought, with the same number of picks, each checked at the current level and sheet. Core sets no limit; when, how often, and at what cost is at the game's or the GM's discretion. [A]
