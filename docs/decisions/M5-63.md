# M5-63 Perk ladders and formula trees drawn as Mermaid flowcharts

Date: 2026-11-17

- **Decision:** `ruleset/scripts/data_flowcharts.py` writes `docs/core/perk-ladders.md` and `docs/core/formula-trees.md` from the rows; `test_data_flowcharts.py` fails when either page is stale, as M5-57's diagram test does. [V 2026-11-17]
- **Ladders by category** [A]: one `graph LR` per feature `category` (combat, magic, social, …) holds every perk of that category that has a perk prerequisite or is one, with its gates on a second line (level, skills, attributes). An arrow runs from the prerequisite to the perk, labelled `rank N` when a rank above 1 is needed (M3-14). A prerequisite from another category is drawn as a rounded box. The issue's "a graph per family" is met by the arrows inside a category chart: a family is a connected line in it, and a chart per family would split the 37 linked perks into many tiny charts.
- **Perks on no ladder:** 59 of the 96 perks have no perk before or after them; they are a table under the charts, with their gates, so every perk appears on the page and a lonely one is easy to spot. [V 2026-11-17]
- **Formula trees:** every value in `ruleset/data/` that has `operation` and `arguments` (outside `bases/`) is drawn as a `flowchart TB`: a diamond per operation, a rounded box per input, a square per number. 18 trees today, in `combat/pipeline.json`, `formulas/*.json`, `loot/core.json`, and `progression/core.json`. Module data has none yet. [V 2026-11-17]
