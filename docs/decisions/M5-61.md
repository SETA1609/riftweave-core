# M5-61 Suggested builds: the ranged path, the arcane archer, and the ranger

Date: 2026-11-18

- **Decision:** the ranged sub-paths are **archer** (bows, light armor, evasion), **arbalist** (hand crossbow, shield, medium armor), and **gunslinger** (guns, light armor, evasion). The last two hybrids are the **arcane trickster** (bows and yellow magic, with stealth) and the **ranger** (bows, blades, and survival). Each passes `build_reference.play` from creation to level 30 at the lowest luck roll. This closes M5-47's plan of twelve builds. [V 2026-11-18, `test_builds.py`]
- **Thrown weapons get no build:** the issue offered bow, crossbow, firearm, or thrown. The thrown line has one perk (Thrown Mastery), against the bow's, crossbow's, and gun's Gap Finder, Steady Aim, and Sniper plus a skill perk each. [A]
- **Trickster over archer:** the issue named "arcane archer or trickster". Yellow magic has a mastery perk (Illusionist) and the trickery spells (Blur, Blinding Flash, Veil, Charm Person); a red-magic archer would repeat the magic swordsman's fire half. [A]
- **How the perks were picked:** as in M5-60: an ordered wish list per build, and each level takes the first wish legal then by the same `play` check the test runs; when none is legal, a general perk, then any perk. [A]
- **Gaps (no perk worth taking at a level; none invented here):**
  - **The ranged table is thin.** Every ranged skill shares Gap Finder, Steady Aim, and Sniper; each has at most one perk of its own (Fast Reload, Hip Shot, Thrown Mastery), and there is no bow perk at all. After Sniper at 18 no ranged build has a ranged perk left to take.
  - **Fillers from the magic table:** with the general table spent, the archer takes Arcane Reservoir three times and Battle Mage at 27 to 30, and the gunslinger Arcane Reservoir, Battle Mage, and Quick Caster. They are legal (INT 6) and useless to a non-caster: a sign of the gap, not a style.
  - **Filler skills:** every build raises Medicine, Insight, Persuasion, Lockpick, or Sleight of Hand to 30 or 40 late, to make general perks legal.
  - **No ranged defense perk:** the arbalist borrows the shield-bearer's block ladder; the archer and the gunslinger the duelist's evasion ladder.
  - **The ranger** reaches Bows 89 and Blades 60: two top skills share 15 points a level at INT 5.
- **Unverified numbers:** the pages quote the perks' own descriptions; how strong a level-30 ranged attack is, is M5-50's (pacing) to check. [U]
