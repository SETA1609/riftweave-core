# M3-24 Combat maneuvers: actions that apply effects for AP and stamina, not mana

Date: 2026-11-17

- **Decision:** a `maneuvers` collection (`core:maneuver/<key>`). A row names the action it performs (`modifies`, today only `attack`), extra `ap` (negative for a faster one; the total never below 1), an optional `mana`, an `attack` add and a `damage_multiplier` as a called shot has, the `ranges` and `weapon_properties` it needs, and `effects` from the shared pool, applied on a hit that is not evaded. The combat command takes `maneuver`, checks it, and returns `ap`, `stamina`, and `effects`. [V 2026-11-17, six combat vectors in Python and Go]
- **Names are our own** [V owner 2026-11-16, copyright-clean names]: the starting rows are **Heavy Swing** (attack + 1 AP, −10, ×1.5, melee, staggers 4 s) and **Quick Strike** (attack − 1 AP, ×0.5, melee with a light weapon). The issue's "power attack" and "quick attack" were working names.
- **The numbers are assumptions** [A]: ×1.5 for one more AP and −10 roughly matches the head called shot (−20, ×1.5) for AP instead of accuracy; ×0.5 for one AP less keeps two quick strikes from beating one attack (2 × 0.5 against 1, before DR, which hits small blows harder). M5-50 (pacing) should check them.
- **The `maneuver` effect channel** is added to the effect schema and to `CHANNEL_REQUIREMENTS`; `stagger` declares it. A maneuver naming an effect without it fails validation. [V 2026-11-17, `test_validate.py`]
- **Every maneuver needs a perk** [V owner 2026-11-18]: a character may attempt a maneuver only while holding a perk that lists it in `unlocks` (which now accepts `core:maneuver/...`). This replaces the first draft, where a maneuver no perk unlocked was open to everyone. Wind-Up (STR 5) unlocks Heavy Swing and Snap Cut (AGI 5) unlocks Quick Strike [A: the gates]. They give no skill bonus [V owner 2026-11-18: skills grow mainly by level-up points and `fortify_skill` from potions, food, and enchanted gear, so a perk's skill bonus must cover two or more skills or be at least +10 (M5-78); a +5 on one skill is not worth a perk], so `test_perks.py` now accepts a perk that only unlocks something. `validate.py` fails on a maneuver no perk unlocks. [V 2026-11-18, `test_validate.py`] Monsters list maneuvers and need no perk.
- **Rider perks and trip/disarm are M3-31** [V owner 2026-11-18]: perks that put a maneuver's effect on every normal attack, one rider at a time (the latest bought replaces the one before, no refund), and the perk-only respec a GM or a game can grant.
- **`power_attack` stays a passive perk** [A]: M3-28 renames it `full_weight` and keeps its passive damage bonus, so it does not become the unlock for Heavy Swing. Unlocking a maneuver is left to M3-28's ladders.
- **Monsters** list `maneuvers` on rows and templates (a template's are appended, skipping duplicates). The troll has Heavy Swing. Template composition lives only in Python (`validate.py`); the Go `monster_sheet` takes a composed row and does not show maneuvers yet. [V 2026-11-17, `test_bestiary.py`]
- **Not done here:** the heavy-weapon surcharge that `brutal_charge` refers to (`heavy_power_attack_surcharge_ignored`) is still pending, as M5-18 listed it; trip, disarm, and shove are left for later rows. The `cleave` effect key and the "power attacks" wording in `brutal_charge` are M3-28's and M5-67's to rename.
- **Overlap:** M3-28 changes the attack cost by loadout. A maneuver's AP is the action's cost plus its own, so it follows whatever cost M3-28 sets.
