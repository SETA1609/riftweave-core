# M2-03 Multi-level example characters as vectors

Date: 2026-11-06

- **Decision:** the record is data, `ruleset/data/examples/characters.json`
  (`character_example.schema.json`): point-buy, race, background, tag skills, and per level
  the luck roll, the perk, the ranks bought, and gear and spells gained. Vectors cannot hold
  it, since a vector may only carry its command's input. **[V 2026-11-06]**
- **Decision:** `ruleset/scripts/test_characters.py` replays the record and checks each
  choice (perk tier, attribute, skill, and prerequisite-perk gates, ranks, the pool and its
  costs, the cap). It generates 40 vectors (`derived` and `progression level_up`, per level)
  and the two tables in `character-creation.md`; the tests fail if either differs. The
  expected stats come from the closed forms, not the reference.
  **[V 2026-11-06, `python -m unittest ruleset/scripts/test_characters.py`; the vectors pass in
  Python and Go]**
- **Decision:** a level up takes the perk before spending the pool, as the level-up procedure
  in `progression.md` lists it, so a perk's skill gate reads the skills as they stood. **[A]**
- **Decision:** the branch stacks on M2-01: nine level-up perks for a dwarf with STR 7, AGI 4,
  and CHA 4 need the M2-01 catalogue. **[V 2026-11-06]**
- **Why:** single-level examples could not show whether the level curve holds.
- **Open:** the per-level grant in `progression.md` ("Hit Points: `END × 8 + 4`" each level)
  disagrees with the closed form in `derived/core.json` (`15 + END × 8 + 4 × level`); the
  record follows the data. One of them is wrong **[U]**. Sefa's three tag skills reach the cap
  of 100 by level 9 (a pool of 23 to 28 at INT 9, one point a rank): the skill curve is fast
  for a high-INT character **[U]**. The level-1 Lira example still says a human has no ability
  modifiers, but `races/core.json` gives CHA +1 and LCK +1 **[U]**.
