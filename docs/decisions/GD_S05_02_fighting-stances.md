# GD_S05_02_fighting-stances Fighting stances: toggles a perk unlocks

Date: 2026-11-19

- **Decision:** a `stances` collection (`ruleset/data/stances/core.json`, `stance.schema.json`), not stance-typed features. A perk with the stance's key names `core:stance/<key>` in its `unlocks` (the maneuver pattern), and `validate.py` fails a stance no perk unlocks, or a stance modifier whose origin is not `core:origin/martial`, whose source is not `stance:<key>`, or whose duration is not `while_applied`. A row holds the loadout it needs and its modifiers; the AP rules (0 at the start of the own turn, 1 mid-turn) and the parry divisor (4) are the file's `rules`. [A]
- **A new `stance` command and kernel function** (Python and Go) runs the toggle: take, change, end, and a loadout change that ends a stance it no longer fits. It returns the active stance's modifiers, which the caller passes to `combat` (defender) and `attack_sequence` (attacker), as perks already are. `combat` and `attack_sequence` do not take a stance key, so a stance needs no second code path. [A]
- **Loadout skills:** "a one-handed blade", "a spear", and "a dagger" are weapon skills, not loadout terms, so the `loadout` definition gains optional `main_skill` and `off_skill` (skill refs), and a stance row may name `main_skills` and `off_skills`. The rapier and the dagger are `piercing`, so Fencer's Measure and Espada y Daga's main hand accept Blades or Piercing, and Espada y Daga's off hand needs a piercing light weapon. [A]
- **Coexistence:** a fighting stance stands together with M3-27's dodge or block stance. Dodge and block are declared defenses that spend AP from the pool; a fighting stance is how the hands are held. Buckler Play only acts while blocking, which needs both. [A]
- **Parry:** a `parry` grant reads the defender's new `weapon_skill` input: a band of `floor(weapon_skill / 4)` under the block band, physical hits only, none for an instant attack, and a crit always hits. A margin in it is `blocked` with the new result field `parried`; `parry_rate` is the band. A parry grant without `weapon_skill` is an error. Espada y Daga's "+10 after a parry" is the `attack_sequence` input `after_parry` (the caller knows whether a hit was parried since the last strike) and adds to the first main-hand strike only. [A]
- **New targets:** `evasion_band` (applies after the dodge doubling and Crossed Guard, never below 0), `parry`, `block_with_weapon`, `off_hand_parries`, `attack_after_parry`, `carry_on_crit`, and three pending ones: `full_weight_doubled` (Full Weight's `melee_damage` still has no kernel step), `opportunity_attack_cost` and `shield_cover_to_ally` (no kernel step has opportunity attacks or cover yet). The existing pending `attack_single_opponent` (Duelist's) is reused for Fencer's Measure and is no longer pending; `unarmed_damage` is read by `attack_sequence` on an empty main hand and is no longer pending. Fighting-stance `attack_roll` adds to every strike. [A]
- **Long Guard's crit carry:** a crit that leaves its foe standing spends a carry as a drop would: the next strike of the action, or a free strike after the last, goes to the first other foe still standing, and a full attack's later strikes follow it. [A]
- **Perk gates:** the issue's levels, plus Espada y Daga AGI 6 and Blades or Piercing 40, Buckler Play AGI 5 and Shields 30, Phalanx END 6 and Piercing 40, Long Guard STR 6 and a melee skill 50, Bare Knuckle END 5 and Unarmed 30, Forward Guard STR 5, Low Guard AGI 5. Fencer's Measure keeps Duelist's gate (Lv12, AGI 7, Blades 60). [A]
- **Renames:** the perk `duelist` is `fencers_measure`; the build `duelist` is `fencer` (key and sub_path), since the issue asks that `duelist` exist as no key. Prose that says "duelist" as a plain word (the rapier's description, the do-not-use list in CONTRIBUTING.md) stays.
- **Not here:** opportunity attacks and shield cover (Phalanx's two targets stay pending), Full Weight's doubling (pending with `melee_damage`), the bash (`boss_strike`, GD_S05_03), and no `change_stance` row in `actions/core.json`: the AP lives once, in `stances/core.json`'s `rules`. The playtest driver is not rerun: Duelist's only modifier was pending, so no build plays differently.
- **Verified:** 14 `stance`, 10 new `combat`, and 9 new `attack_sequence` vectors pass in Python (`test_vectors`) and in Go (`go test ./...`, every vector through the core, the CLI, the server, WASM, and MCP); `validate.py`, `test_validate.py`, the unit tests, the balance gate, gofmt, and go vet pass. [V 2026-11-19]
- **Not done:** `frontend/src/api/openapi.d.ts` is not regenerated (it needs a running server), as in GD_S05_01.
- **Differences from the issue:** the `stances` collection, its schema, the `stance` command (`backend/internal/stance/`, `backend/contract/stance.json`, `stance.json` vectors), the Go loader, `combat` and `attack_sequence` changes, and the build rename are beyond the issue's ~12 files; `actions/core.json` needed no change.
- **Risk: over the 20-file rule.** As in GD_S05_01, splitting the ruleset half from the backend half would leave Go's vector tests failing on the new vector file, and the generated pages (`contract-diagram.md`, `data-model-diagram.md`, `perk-ladders.md`) and the build docs add files of their own. Kept as one pull request.
