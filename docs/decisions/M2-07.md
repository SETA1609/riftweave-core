# M2-07 Effects over time and food

Date: 2026-11-11

- **Decision:** the tick rule is in `docs/core/modifier.md#effects-over-time`. An advance is in seconds, and a turn is `seconds_per_turn` seconds, so a tick covers only the seconds that remain and the total matches real time. **[V 2026-11-11, `consume` vectors in Python and Go]**
- **Decision:** `seconds_per_turn` is 5, the owner's value in the M3-11 scope. This issue had to fix it, so M3-11 finds it already in `actions/core.json`. **[V 2026-11-11, owner decision]**
- **Decision:** `burn` and `poison_health_drain` stay effects under the same rule and do not become conditions. **[A]**
- **Decision:** using the same item again restarts its own effects and does not stack them. **[A]**
- **Why:** `schema.json` said seconds, `contract.md` and `modifier.md` said turns, and nothing ran a tick.
- **Difference from the issue:** a new kernel function, `consume`, holds the tick and the food policy, because neither `modifiers` nor `conditions` tracks time in seconds or a resource pool.
- **Open:** only `restore_resource` ticks a pool through `consume`. Damage and drain over time on a spell or a poison have the same rule, but no consumer runs them yet. **[U]**
