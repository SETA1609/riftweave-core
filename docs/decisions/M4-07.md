# M4-07 Loot composes items: default materials and size

Date: 2026-11-08

- **Decision:** a loot row's `material` lands on an item whose `default_materials` include it;
  otherwise the item drops in its first default material, and its value follows the material
  that landed. The steel hoard of the iron colossus yields a steel breastplate and an elven
  wood longbow, never a steel bow. Spec: `docs/core/loot.md#what-lands-on-an-item`.
  **[V 2026-11-08, loot vectors in Python and Go]**
- **Decision:** a row with no `material` leaves its items as listed, at 100 percent; they do
  not take their first default. So no table's or monster's budget moved. An item with no
  `default_materials` keeps the row's material. **[A]**
- **Decision:** a loot row's `size` applies to weapons only: dice one rung down or up
  (`combat/dice_ladder.json`), the size's attack (+10, −10), and the size's value percent.
  Value is `material percent × size percent / 100`, rounded down, in both references.
  Giants (`giant`, and `frost_giant` with steel) drop oversized weapons, the goblin
  skirmisher undersized ones; every monster still fits its budget. **[V 2026-11-08,
  `test_loot.py`]**
- **Decision:** the elven wood longbow lists its own `default_materials` (`elven_wood`), so
  a named item can be found only in its own material. `validate.py` strips
  `default_materials` after the overrides merge, not before. **[V 2026-11-08]**
- **Difference from the issue:** it asked for "a steel sword and a wooden bow" from the steel
  hoard; no hoard holds a sword, and its bow is the named elven wood longbow, so the vector
  shows a steel breastplate and an elven wood bow.
- **Why:** M2-05 made materials Elin-like defaults and sizes composed variants, but loot put
  any material on anything and had no way to yield a sized weapon.
- **Open:** merchant stock does not compose items yet **[U]**. Drops still live in the loot
  file; M4-08 moves them onto the monster **[U]**.
