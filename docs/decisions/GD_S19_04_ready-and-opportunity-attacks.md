# GD_S19_04_ready-and-opportunity-attacks Ready an attack on reserved AP, and opportunity attacks in the pipeline

Date: 2026-11-23

- **Decision:** an opportunity attack and a readied attack are both one attack of the loadout out of turn, run by `attack_sequence` as the actions `opportunity` and `readied`, but paid differently: an opportunity attack is a reaction (no AP), a readied attack is held AP (no reaction). The rule is [combat.md#attacks-out-of-turn](../core/combat.md#attacks-out-of-turn), the `ready` row is in [actions.md](../core/actions.md#costs).
- **Owner decisions kept** (2026-11-19): opportunity attacks are a base rule that perks extend; counters stay perks (Riposte); a readied attack is paid from held AP at the loadout's attack cost, which replaces modules/combat.md's flat 3 AP from the carried pool.
- **Nothing spent when it does not fire:** a readied attack whose trigger never happens or with too little held, and an opportunity attack a Disengage prevents or with no reaction left, spend no AP and no reaction. The result says why (`reason`). [V 2026-11-23, `attack_sequence` vectors in Python and Go]
- **The guard drop is in the result** for a readied attack (`guard_lost`, the cost times `guard_per_ap`), and the `actions` command's `held_spent` already lowers the guard and the carry the same way (GD_S19_05). An opportunity attack loses no guard.
- **Phalanx** adds a reaction a round (`add reactions_per_round 1`, owner 2026-11-23). It lowered `opportunity_attack_cost` by 1 until opportunity attacks became free; that target is removed.
- **What the kernel does not track:** reach and who left it, one readied attack at a time, and the readied attack ending when the actor moves or is knocked down. The caller (a session or a table) says what happened (`event`, `disengaged`); the command decides whether it fires and what it costs. [A]
- **Opportunity attacks are reactions** (owner, 2026-11-23): they cost no AP. Everyone has `reactions_per_round` (1, in `actions/core.json`'s pool) between two of their turns; the caller passes `reactions_used`, and one past the most does not fire (`no_reaction_left`). Watchful Blade grants `reactions_from_agi`, which makes the most max(1, AGI − 5); Phalanx then adds 1. A readied attack spends no reaction (owner's choice over sharing the count), so AP-based overwatch and reaction-based opportunity attacks are two budgets, and a reaction-built character is a real choice.
- **Real time** (owner, 2026-11-23): reactions are the table rule; a real-time engine fires opportunity attacks automatically or leaves them out.
- **Assumed** [A]: Riposte does not count against the round (it is a counter of the dodge stance, run by the caller as its own attack); the perk Watchful Blade (level 6, AGI 7, ungated) grants `reactions_from_agi`; its tier and prerequisites are a first guess.
- **Open:** ranged overwatch waits for GD_S17_05; the triggers that perks may add (casting, drinking, or standing up in reach) are [A].
