# GD_S05_03_defense-perk-ladders Defense perk ladders: shields, shield bash, evasion, no armor, and armor on casting

Date: 2026-11-19

- **Decision:** a tiered perk is one row with `ranks` and a new `rank_levels` (the level each tier opens at). `take_perk` (Python and Go) reads `rank_levels[rank held]` as the next tier's level gate in place of `prerequisite.level`, so the build legality test and the playtest driver enforce it with no change of their own. `validate.py` checks that a row's gates number its ranks, that the first gate is its prerequisite level (or 1), and that each step is 3 to 6 levels; it also replays each build's `perks_by_level` and fails a tier taken before its gate. [A]
- **Non-linear tiers:** Shield Training's +2 / +3 / +4 is not value × tier, so a row may hold `rank_values` (target to one value per tier), read by `emit_perk` and Go's `Feature.EmitAt`. Every other ladder is linear and uses the modifier's value times the tier. [A]
- **Spell Ward** is a tier (`add spell_ward 1`), not a flag. While blocking, a blockable (`single` or `area`) magic hit whose margin lands in the full block band is **warded**: it still hits, and after resist it loses `floor(damage × ward_percent / 100)`, with `ward_percent = min(END × 2 × tier, 50)`. `pipeline.json` holds the formula (`spell_ward_percent`), the cap, and the 10 × 3 `spell_ward_table`, which `validate.py` checks against the formula. The half-width magic band (`magic_block_rate`, `block_magic`) is gone. A crit is never warded, and an instant spell meets no band. The result gains `warded` and `ward_percent`. [A]
- **Shield Wall** adds `block_band` (3 per tier) to the block band; `block_reduction` is gone. **Unarmored Grace** adds `unarmored_evasion_band` (5 per tier) only when the new `defender.armor` input is `none`, which is its default. **Armored Evasion** is +5 Evasion per tier. [A]
- **Boss Strike (the bash):** `attack_sequences/core.json` gains a `shield_bash` row: the off hand (a shield or a buckler) reads as a `light_weapon`, so the jab and the attack follow the light weapon row (a jab at -10 for 2 AP; the attack's off-hand strike at -20). From tier 2 an attack with a shield always includes the bash, so one-handed plus shield costs the light row's AP. The bash rolls on `off_skill` (the Shields skill), deals `shield_dr` plus its roll, bludgeoning, and needs the new `attacker.shield_dr` input. Tier 3 adds `stagger_ap` 2 on a bash that hits; tier 4 makes a bash jab 1 AP when the new `after_block` input is set. A bash strike reports `bash: true` and `stagger_ap`. [A]
- **Casting:** `armor_penalty` is none 0, light -5, medium -15, heavy -30, and a required `shield_penalty` is none 0, buckler 0, standard -5, tower -15, read from the new `caster.shield` input. Mailed Casting (`multiply armor_cast_penalty 0.75`, so ×0.75 / ×0.5 / ×0.25) scales each penalty on its own, rounded down, so heavy armor at tier 1 is -23. [A]
- **Guardian and Flowing Step** are rows with pending targets (`guardian`, `flowing_step`): no kernel step has allies, cover, or opportunity attacks yet. The tower shield's half cover is removed from its description and points to Guardian. [U]
- **Removed:** `acrobat` and its `dodge_stamina` target; `battle_mage` is `mailed_casting` with every ref (five builds, the docs). Builds that took Acrobat take Low Guard or Forward Guard (archer, gunslinger, ranger). The arbalist takes Shield Training tier 2 at level 6, since Bulwark now needs it.
- **Melee builds:** the shield-bearer takes every shield ladder (Shield Training, Shield Wall, Boss Strike to tier 3 or 4, Spell Ward and Guardian to tier 3); the two-handed fighter and the fencer take the GD_S05_01 and GD_S05_02 ladders (Follow Up, Reaping Arc, Circle Cut, Press On, Long Guard, Forward and Low Guard) and Armored Evasion's three tiers. That closes the "fillers from level 25" gaps of M5-47 for the shield-bearer and narrows them for the others.
- **Tier gates do not apply to monster perks:** a monster row is composed, not levelled. [A]
