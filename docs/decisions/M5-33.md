# M5-33 Percentages are `mul`, and distances are units

Date: 2026-11-11

- **Decision:** a share of a number is a `mul` on that number, so +25% is `mul 1.25`. A ranked `mul` of `m` emits `1 + (m − 1) × rank`, so a perk gives the number it gave as an `add`. Spec: `docs/modules/progression.md`. **[V 2026-11-11, `test_modifier_units.py`]**
- **Decision:** prices and resistances have nothing to scale yet, so they stay `add` with the unit in their row's description. **[A]**
- **Decision:** darkvision (9 and 18 became 6 and 12) and the safe fall (6 became 4) are converted at 1.5 m a unit, matching the perks' own text. **[V 2026-11-11]**
- **Why:** there were two ways to scale a number, and three targets stored metres.
- **Difference from the issue:** four targets needed new names because the plain name collides with another row or reads badly: `block_reduction`, `detection_chance`, `unarmed_strike_damage`, and `spell_cost`, which the casting rules already read.
- **Risk:** two different `mul` on one target now multiply, where two percentages added. Each perk alone gives its old number; two together give slightly more or less (+25% and +50% were +75%, and are now ×1.875). **[A]**
