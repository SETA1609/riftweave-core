# M3-17 Monster attack and defense skills grow with level

Date: 2026-11-14

- **Decision:** one kernel rate, `monster_skill_growth` in `progression/core.json`, not a `skillPerLevel` per creature type. The issue offered either; the gap it fixes is between monsters and players, and the player's pace is one formula, so the monster's is one number too. A creature type that should fight better or worse writes its skills, or a later issue adds a per-type rate. [A]
- **Decision:** `per_level` is 17: the pool of an average actor (INT 5, LCK 5, so `5 + 5 × 2 + 2.5` on an average luck roll) spent on one tag skill at 1 point a rating, rounded down. A monster with AGI 5 reads evasion 15 at level 1, 83 at level 5, and 100 from level 6; the same player's main skill reads 15, 85, and 100. The gap is at most 15 at every level from 1 to 30. [V 2026-11-14, `test_bestiary.py`]
- **Decision:** only the `combat` and `magic` categories grow (the weapon skills, `evasion`, `block`, the armor skills, and every `<color>_magic`): those are the attack, defense, and spell skills the issue names. Stealth, social, knowledge, survival, and utility skills stay at `skill_start`. A grown skill stops at `skill_cap` (100), as a bought one does; a modifier can still pass it. A written skill is the author's value and does not grow. [V 2026-11-14, `monster_sheet` vectors]
- **Decision:** the growth is read on the sheet (`monster_sheet`, Python and Go), from the row's `level`, not written into the row by composition. A summoned or generated monster at the caster's level therefore grows by the same rule with no extra step. [V 2026-11-14]
- **Why:** found by the M5-18 agent runs: monsters fought at `skill_start` (about 25 to 35) at every level, hit 24 to 32% of their attacks, and won none of 240 fights.
- **Open:** a monster's damage still grows only by `damagePerLevel` (0.25), and M5-50 may slow a player's skill pace; if it does, `per_level` should follow it. Whether level-matched fights now land in the hits-to-kill bands is for the balance pass (M5-09) or a rerun of the M5-18 driver. [U]
- **Risk:** by level 6 or 7 every monster has 100 in every combat and magic skill, so a level-8 wolf reads 100 in `red_magic` and `guns` too. It casts and shoots only with what its row lists, so the numbers are unused, but a reader of the sheet may be surprised. [A]
