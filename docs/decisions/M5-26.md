# M5-26 References are typed by category

Date: 2026-11-10

- **Decision:** `schema.json` has one typed reference per category, plus `itemRef` for the five
  equipment categories. A recipe input's `key` keeps the general reference, because it may
  really hold a weapon, an armor, a material, a gem, or a crafted item. Spec:
  `docs/core/integrity.md`. **[V 2026-11-10, `validate.py` and `test_integrity.py`]**
- **Decision:** `parameter_category` on an effect says what kind its `parameter` must reference.
  It is set on `fortify_skill` and `bound_weapon`. **[V 2026-11-10]**
- **Why:** a field that needed a monster accepted any reference, and a free `parameter` let
  summons name a plain word.
- **Found:** three accessories used a bare skill word in `fortify_skill`; they now use references.
- **Open:** `summon` gets `parameter_category: summon` once M3-08 merges, since its parameters
  on `main` are still plain words.
