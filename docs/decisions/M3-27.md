# M3-27 One attack roll: defense is a band, a shield is DR, block and dodge are declared stances

Date: 2026-11-16
Modified: 2026-11-16

- **Decision:** an attack takes one d100 roll. The defender's evasion is a band of the attacker's
  margin, `floor(evasion / 2)`: a success whose margin is under it is evaded (a single attack does
  nothing, an area attack is halved). A carried shield's DR adds to armor DR against physical
  types with no action. Dodge (3 AP) is a stance that doubles the band; block (2 AP, reserved) is a
  stance that adds a band of `floor(shields / 2)` under it, where a hit is blocked for no damage.
  A crit always hits; instant meets no band. **[V 2026-11-16, `validate.py`, the unit tests,
  `go test ./...`, and the frontend tests]**
- **Reverses two owner decisions of 2026-11-04,** at the owner's request (2026-11-16): the
  defender's reaction roll ("Evasion is a defender reaction after the attack hits, contested like
  block"), and "the TTRPG passive-penalty variant is dropped". The passive defense returns as a
  band on the attacker's own roll, not a penalty on the target, so the target is still only the
  attacker's numbers and "evaded" stays its own outcome.
- **Owner picks:** dodge is option a, a stance until the next turn (the other options made play
  more complex without a passive option); an area attack in the band is still halved while
  dodging ("keep the area damage").
- **Assumptions, from the issue:** both rates are half the skill; the dodge doubles the rate;
  Spell Ward's band is half the width; Bulwark staggers for 2 AP (`bulwark_stagger_ap`), since a
  block already does no damage; the Riposte and Bulwark windows are the band's lowest rolls (its
  highest margins, next to the hit band), as wide as the defender's crit window. All are formula
  trees or numbers in `combat/pipeline.json`. **[A]**
- **Differences from the issue:**
  - A block stance with no `shield_dr` or `shields_skill` is an error, and an unknown stance is an
    error, both checked after the situations; the contract leaves `stance` a free string so the
    error vector stays a valid input.
  - A defender with no `evasion_skill` has a band of 0, so a vector that is not about defense can
    leave it out.
  - A blocked hit lists no called-shot riders, like an evade; the old Bulwark kept them because a
    negated hit was still a hit.
  - Piercing halves armor and shield DR together.
  - The issue named `evasion_rate.json` and `block_rate.json` under `formulas/`; the trees sit in
    `combat/pipeline.json` beside the other pipeline trees, one file instead of two.
- **Vectors:** the 45 vectors that rolled a reaction are replaced by 54 band vectors, including the
  rewritten worked examples of `docs/modules/combat.md` §9. Of the others, one changes on purpose
  ("evasion does not lower the attacker's target": margin 2 now lands in the band of 45), and one
  that is about the attribute modifier lowers the defender's evasion from 25 to 20 so its margin
  10 stays a hit. The two casting vectors with an evade drop the reaction and keep their results.
  `reaction_tags` is gone from every expectation.
- **Not done:** armor and encumbrance penalties on the evasion skill are still not in the vectors
  [U]; the human CLI's attack form has no stance field (it never had a reaction); the Playwright
  run was not repeated, as its attack test uses the first vector, which did not change.
- **Risk:** breaking for every caller that sends `defender.reaction` or reads `reaction_tags`: the
  frontend and the API types are updated here; a saved playtest session that recorded a reaction
  input is history and is not replayed by any test. Balance moves: at equal skill a defender now
  evades about as often as before (two fighters at 100 still split hits and dodges about evenly),
  but a block now stops a hit outright, so block bands may need tuning in the M5-50 pacing pass.
  **[A]**
