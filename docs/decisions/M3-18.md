# M3-18 A cast's damage resolves through the combat pipeline

Date: 2026-11-15
Modified: 2026-11-15

- **Decision:** a `casting` call whose `target` has `hp` runs each `core:effect/damage_<type>`
  effect through the combat function's steps 2 to 8, with the cast check standing in for the
  attack roll (the same target, roll, and LCK, so the same margin, crit, and fumble). The result
  lists each packet (`amount`, `damage`, `evaded`, `blocked`) and the target's `hp` after them.
  Without `hp` the effects come back as authored, so every earlier vector holds.
  **[V 2026-11-15, eight `casting` vectors in Python and Go]**
- **Why reuse combat:** one place subtracts resist and DR and reads the reaction, so a fire bolt
  and a sword lose HP the same way, and the M5-18 driver stops doing it by hand.
- **Finding:** no spell damage type takes armor DR (each is tagged `bypasses_armor` or
  `bypasses_physical_armor`), so worn armor never reduces spell damage; a vector pins it.
  **[V 2026-11-15]**
- **Decision:** a crit cast doubles its damage, because an offensive spell is an attack
  (`combat.md`). An effect with a duration over one second is not applied by the cast; no
  damaging spell has one. **[A]**
- **Decision:** orange, green, and violet having no damaging spell is intended: orange fights
  through summons, green heals and grows, violet charms and deceives. **[A]**
- **Difference from the issue:** the target's save `roll` is no longer required by the
  contract, since a non-instant spell's target needs only `hp`; an instant spell with a target
  and no `roll` is an error.
- **Open:** the session does not fill the target from a stored monster yet; a client passes
  `hp`, `resist`, and the rest. **[A]**
