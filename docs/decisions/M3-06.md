# M3-06 Spell knowledge, template spells, and uncraftable effects

Date: 2026-11-11

- **Decision:** a character knows a set of effect refs. Learning a spell teaches its craftable effects, not its target shape; Research teaches one effect for 7 days and 50 gp and one `<color>_magic` check, refused below the effect's craft threshold (magnitude 1, instant, touch). Spec: `docs/modules/magic.md#spell-knowledge`. **[A]** **[V 2026-11-11, `spellcraft` vectors in Python and Go]**
- **Decision:** `craftable: false` on `teleport`, `mark`, `recall`, `storage` (four new effects), `bound_weapon`, and `summon`; `restore_resource` lists `uncraftable_parameters: ["mana"]`. Absorb and drain effects stay craftable. **[V 2026-11-11, `test_spells.py`]**
- **Decision:** `spellcraft` is a new kernel function with three ops (`craft`, `learn`, `research`), with a contract and Go code in this change, as `CONTRIBUTING.md` requires for a new function. A recipe is checked per effect in the order channel, uncraftable, excluded parameter, unknown, off color; its price is `mana`, so `cost` means only the Research gold. Thresholds round a half up, in both languages. Spec: `docs/core/casting.md#spellcraft`.
- **Decision:** `world` reaches a place anywhere, with no line of sight or range. Recall and Divine Intervention use it; Mark and Pocket Storage are `self`. Spec: `docs/core/casting.md#the-world-target`. **[A]**
- **Decision:** the templates are Fire Bolt (bolt), Holy Light (touch), Fire Burst (burst), Conjured Barrier (ward), and Mend Wounds (mend). **[V 2026-11-11, a test crafts each one and gets its mana and cast threshold]**
- **Difference from the issue:** Divine Intervention has no faction `parameter`. Core has no deities, and the factions module has none either, so the row is written once and a game with deities writes one row per deity with its faction ref.
- **Difference from the issue:** the ward template is Conjured Barrier, not Ward, because Ward is a concentration spell and a template is plain (the vector-fix commit says so).
- **Difference from the issue:** the Research numbers are under `research` in `casting/core.json`, not in `ruleset/data/actions/`: the actions table holds AP actions in a turn, and Research takes days.
- **Why:** spellcrafting had no limit on what a character could compose, so mana regen and teleport could be crafted from nothing.
- **Open:** the stored actor (`docs/core/state.md`) has no known effects yet, so a session cannot keep them; the `spellcraft` command has no session adapter. The research numbers and the new effects' base costs (teleport 100, mark 50, recall 100, storage 40) are untested in play. The minimal craft threshold is low for most effects (1 for fire), so the Research gate rarely refuses. **[U]**
