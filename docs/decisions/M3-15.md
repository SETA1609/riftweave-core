# M3-15 Monsters and NPCs take perks

Date: 2026-11-14

- **Decision:** a monster row or template holds `perks`, a list of `{ "perk": "core:feature/<key>", "rank": n }` (`heldPerk` in `schema.json`), the rank 1 when left out. One shape for rows, templates, and the `monster_sheet` input. [V 2026-11-14]
- **Decision:** a perk emits its modifiers at its rank by the one rule players use, now `data.Feature.EmitAt` in Go (shared with the session's fill of APP-13) and `emit_perk` in Python. `monster_sheet` applies them to each derived stat, to an authored `hitPoints` (the troll's 100 reads 130 with Toughness at rank 2), and to each skill it reads, written or not. [V 2026-11-14, `monster_sheet` vectors in Python and Go]
- **Decision:** a template's perks merge into the monster's as its spells do: added, and when both name a perk the higher rank is kept. A row's own `perks` in its overrides land after the templates and replace the list unless written with `$extend`, as for spells. [V 2026-11-14, `test_bestiary.py`]
- **Decision:** prerequisites are not checked on a monster. A rank past the perk's `ranks` fails validation; a `social` or `utility` perk prints a warning and passes. [V 2026-11-14]
- **Decision:** examples: the troll holds Toughness at rank 2, the bandit captain Armored Evasion and Alert, `dire` grants Toughness, `elder` Second Wind. The balance report reads a row's authored HP, not its sheet, so these perks do not move its bands. [A]
- **Why:** owner request, 2026-11-12: monsters, mobs, and NPCs can hold perks to empower them.
- **Open:** the branch builds on APP-13 for the shared emitter; it merges after APP-13. Whether a perk's HP should count in the hits-to-kill bands is for the balance pass (M5-09). [A]
