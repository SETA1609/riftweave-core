# M5-48 Balance checks for potions, magic, and scrolls

Date: 2026-11-12

- **Decision:** toxicity is a formula and a static value (owner decision 2026-11-12). `formulas/toxicity.json` is `instant_cost + floor(over_time_cost / 2)`: the spell cost formula on the drink's effects with target `self`, an over-time pool restore counting half. 40 HP at once is 20; the same 40 over 20 seconds is 10. A stock drink keeps its static `toxicity`, at most `toxicity.tolerance` (5) from the formula. **[V 2026-11-12, `formula` vectors, `balance_report.py --gate`]** The spell cost as the basis and the half for over time are **[A]**.
- **Decision:** the measure for "a stock potion is weaker than magic" is per point of toxicity at magic skill 50 (owner decision 2026-11-12): a Potion of Mana spent on the best spell restoring the same pool, paid with the skill discount, must restore more per toxicity than the stock drink. A pool no spell restores (mana) is not compared. Gold per HP is not checked. **[A]** for leaving gold out.
- **Decision:** the levers: the Potion of Mana goes from 20 to the formula's 8 toxicity, so the spell route gives 5 HP a point of toxicity against a healing potion's 2; the fire and frost resistance potions go from 120 to 40 seconds (formula 20, the 20 they state), since at 120 seconds the formula gives 60. The other stock drinks were already within 5. **[V 2026-11-12]**
- **Decision:** scrolls are new: `type: scroll`, `delivery: read`, and `consumable.spell`, with no effects and no toxicity. A stock scroll costs at least `scroll_gp_per_mana` (5) gold per mana of its spell; `scroll_fire_bolt` is 40 gp for a 6-mana spell. **[A]** for the rate.
- **Decision:** four gate checks in `balance/core.json`: `toxicity_formula`, `instant_toxicity_above_over_time`, `stock_potion_below_magic`, `scroll_price`. `balance_report.report` reads consumables and spells from the data unless the caller passes them. The `formula` command takes `variables`, in Python and Go.
- **Why:** the owner's rules (stock potions below magic, instant above over time, scrolls for gold) needed numbers that the data cannot drift from.
- **Open:** the vector `drinks add up` (M2-09) now drinks the healing potion instead of the mana potion, to keep its total at 30. The crafted-potion side (alchemy) and magic items stay with their modules.
