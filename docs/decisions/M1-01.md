# M1-01 Reconcile docs/modules/combat.md with the kernel

Date: 2026-11-04

- **Decision:** `docs/modules/combat.md` holds no rule of its own. An owner table links each
  rule to `docs/core/`; prose rules not yet rows are tagged **[A]** with the issue that turns
  them into rows (M2-06 reactions and parry, M3-03 cover, called shots, crit riders). Real-time
  clients get one presentation section; there are no TTRPG or video-game columns. The two
  worked examples are six `combat` vectors (`example 1a` to `example 2c`). **[V 2026-11-04]**
- **Why:** two authorities for combat. The old file still had bonus actions, a reaction
  slot, a d20 initiative, a crit confirmation roll, and an HP table that did not match
  `derived/core.json` (551 HP at level 30 where the data gives 191 for END 7).
- **Open:**
  - An opportunity attack costs the attacker its normal 3 AP from its carried pool; with
    fewer than 3 carried there is none **[A]**. It replaces the old reaction for it.
  - Initiative ties go to the higher AGI, then a d100 roll-off **[A]**.
  - Encumbrance in `armor.md` uses worn weight against STR × 10, 15, 20; the derived `carry`
    stat is `25 + STR × 10`. Which one is the authority is open **[U]**.
  - The `ttrpg` and `video_game` prose on condition rows is still per-mode; it becomes
    modifiers in M3-03 **[U]**.
