# M4-03 Bestiary to the content floor

Date: 2026-11-06

- **Decision:** 44 monsters (11 before, 33 new) from level 1 to 30, each a base plus overrides.
  Monsters gain `attacks` (damage amount and type, delivery, optional phase), `resist` (flat
  by damage type, the combat pipeline's `defender.resist`), and `role` (standard, boss,
  minion). Spec: `docs/modules/monsters.md#attacks-resistances-and-role`.
  **[V 2026-11-06, `python -m unittest ruleset/scripts/test_bestiary.py`]**
- **Decision:** "every phase used as an attack and as a resistance" reads the phase as
  two-sided: a monster's own `phase` is its resistance side, through the cycles, and an
  attack's `phase` is its attack side. Every one of the 13 damage types is an attack and a
  resistance of some monster. **[A]** for the reading.
- **Decision:** both monster balance checks are gates. The 11 older monsters keep their levels
  (the loot vectors read them) and come into band: HP and the strongest damage per tier, and
  the ember drake, stone golem as bosses (the golem's 110 HP at level 5 was a boss's 20 hits).
  The hits-to-kill band follows the `role`, and the damage check reads attack amounts as
  well as `damage_*` abilities. **[V 2026-11-06, `balance_report.py --gate`]**
- **Decision:** base growth caps an attribute at 10: a level-30 monster on a level-4 base
  grew past the schema's 1 to 10. `validate.py` and `monsters.md` say so. **[V 2026-11-06]**
- **Decision:** each new monster has a drop row on an existing table, within the loot budget
  at its level. **[V 2026-11-06, `test_loot.py`]**
- **Why:** 11 monsters, all below level 8, could not carry levels 1 to 30.
- **Open:** the loot tables stop at level 7, so a level-30 boss rolls the level-7 hoard two or
  three times; tables for the high tiers are a loot issue **[U]**. The combat pipeline does not
  read a monster row yet; the caller copies `attacks` and `resist` into a combat input **[U]**.
  Phase interaction on monster attacks (the wuxing multiplier) is not in the combat pipeline
  **[U]**.
