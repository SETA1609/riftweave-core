# M3-12 An attribute modifier adds to attack damage

Date: 2026-11-11

- **Decision:** an attribute's modifier is `attribute − 5`, once as data, and `damage.ability` adds it to the roll. Spec: `docs/core/combat.md` and `docs/modules/attributes.md#the-attribute-modifier`. **[V 2026-11-11, `combat.json` vectors in Python and Go]**
- **Decision:** melee reads `str`, `finesse` reads `agi`, and a ranged weapon names its attribute by how quick or heavy it is. The author ruled on each named weapon; `blunderbuss`, `bow`, `improvised_bow`, `sling`, and `throwing_knife` are extended from that rule. **[A]**
- **Decision:** the modifier adds before the called-shot multiplier and the crit, so both multiply it. **[A]**
- **Why:** nothing in the pipeline used an attribute for damage, though `attributes.md` said STR governs melee damage.
- **Difference from the issue:** `thrown_rock` needed an `accepted` balance row, because its 1d4 is below the weapon dice band, like the dagger and sling.
- **Open:** no weapon or armor changed for balance. A creation-level main attribute of 8 to 10 with a 1d8 weapon averages 7.5 to 9.5, above the early band of 4 to 7. Check it in play. **[U]**
