# M1-05 Modifier stacking and condition vectors

Date: 2026-11-04

- **Decision:** for one target, modifiers expire, then dedupe by source, target, and op
  (latest wins), then `add`, `mul` (floored), and `set` last; `grant` makes flags that stay
  while any live source grants them; `resist` subtracts from a packet with the matching tag,
  never below zero. An effect event applies every condition that lists it in `appliedBy` and
  removes every one that lists it in `removedBy`; stacks stop at `maxStacks`. Spec:
  `docs/core/modifier.md`. 23 `modifiers` and 57 `conditions` vectors. **[V 2026-11-04]**
- **Why:** the contract named the ops but not their order, and the reference raised an error
  on `set`, `grant`, and `resist`. It also deduped by source and op only, so one source on two
  targets collided; the key now includes the target.
- **Open:** `set` last and `mul` floored are choices **[A]**. `uses` durations are counted by
  the caller and have no vector **[A]**. Removing a stacked condition drops every stack at
  once **[A]**. The `combat` and `effects` prose on condition rows is not modifiers yet
  (M1-01, M3-03) **[U]**.
