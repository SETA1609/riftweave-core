# M1-05 Modifier stacking and condition vectors

Date: 2026-11-06

- **Decision:** for one target, modifiers expire, then dedupe by origin, source, target, and
  op (the strongest wins), then keep the strongest bonus and penalty of each `highest` origin,
  then `add`, `mul` (floored), and `set` last (the latest set wins); `grant` makes flags that stay
  while any live source grants them; `resist` subtracts from a packet with the matching tag,
  never below zero. An effect event applies every condition that lists it in `appliedBy` and
  removes every one that lists it in `removedBy`; stacks stop at `maxStacks`. Spec:
  `docs/core/modifier.md`. 34 `modifiers` and 57 `conditions` vectors. **[V 2026-11-06]**
- **Decision (owner, 2026-11-06):** a modifier may carry an `origin`, a `core:origin/<key>` row
  in `ruleset/data/origins/core.json`: arcane, divine, primal, alchemy, enchantment, martial,
  morale, environment (policy `highest`), and equipment, trained, innate, condition (policy
  `add`). Different origins always stack. Spec: `docs/core/modifier.md#origin`. **[V 2026-11-06]**
- **Why:** the contract named the ops but not their order, and the reference raised an error
  on `set`, `grant`, and `resist`. It also deduped by source and op only, so one source on two
  targets collided; the key now includes the target. A potion and a spell of the same effect
  shared the source `effect:<key>` and replaced each other, while two different potions on one
  stat added. The owner wants the kind of source to decide: a Fortify Strength potion (+10) and a
  Giant Strength potion (+5) give +10; the same potion plus a spell give +20.
- **Open:** `set` last and `mul` floored are choices **[A]**. `uses` durations are counted by
  the caller and have no vector **[A]**. Removing a stacked condition drops every stack at
  once **[A]**. The `combat` and `effects` prose on condition rows is not modifiers yet
  (M1-01, M3-03) **[U]**. Bonuses and penalties of one origin are ranked apart, so one of each
  both count **[A]**. The trained, equipment, innate, and condition origins are `add` so two
  perks or two armor pieces still stack **[A]**. The White school is not mapped to `divine`; a
  row names the origin **[U]**. Emitters do not stamp origins yet: no runner reads `defaultFor`
  and no data row names an `origin` **[U]**. `appliedEffect.durationType` and the modifier
  `stack` field overlap with origins and are left as they are **[U]**.
- **Risk:** the factions module's reference (`ruleset/scripts/faction_reference.py`) keeps its
  own copy of the old stacking (latest wins, no origin, no floor). It is not a kernel runner, so
  no vector catches the drift.
