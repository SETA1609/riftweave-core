# M5-60 Suggested builds: the magic path, and the magic swordsman

Date: 2026-11-17

- **Decision:** the magic sub-paths are **blaster** (red and indigo magic), **summoner** (orange and black), and **support** (green and white, with the bard line); the first hybrid is the **magic swordsman** (blades and red magic). Each passes `build_reference.play` from creation to level 30 at the lowest luck roll. [V 2026-11-17, `test_builds.py`]
- **`spells` on a build** (schema addition, optional): the spells a build leans on, in about the order it learns them. A test checks each is of a color whose `<color>_magic` skill the build raises. The issue asked for "the spells it leans on"; M5-47's schema had no field for them. [A]
- **How the perks were picked:** each build has an ordered wish list (its path's perks, ranks repeated), and each level takes the first wish that is legal then, by the same `play` check the test runs; when no wish is legal, a general perk. So every perk is legal at its level, and the path's perks come as early as the skills allow. [A]
- **Gaps (no perk worth taking at a level; none invented here):**
  - **Above level 20 the magic table runs out for every caster.** The blaster's last path perk is Archmage at 24; levels 19 to 30 except 24 are general perks. The summoner holds out longest (Ancient at 24, Host at 28); the support's last are the bard perks (18, 19, 28).
  - **No per-color mastery past the second rank** of Pyromancer, Healer, and Zealot, and none at all for indigo, orange (beyond summons), or black (beyond Necromancer). A blaster's second school (indigo) has one perk, Telekinetic.
  - **No caster defense perk:** nothing raises evasion or a ward for a caster in robes; Warded Mind and Iron Will are the only ones.
  - **Filler skills:** to make general perks legal late, every build raises Medicine, Insight, Investigation, Repair, Survival, Animal Handling, and Lore to 40 at the end of its priorities. That is a sign of the gap, not a style.
  - **The magic swordsman** never reaches Riposte (Evasion 60): its points go to two top skills.
- **Unverified numbers:** the pages quote the perks' own descriptions; how strong a level-30 blaster's Meteor is, is M5-50's (pacing) to check. [U]
