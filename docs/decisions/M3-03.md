# M3-03 Cover, flanking, terrain, and called shots as modifiers

Date: 2026-11-06

- **Decision:** cover (half, three-quarters, full), flanking, a prone attacker or target, an
  unseen target (a blinded attacker or an invisible defender), and shooting into melee are core
  rows in `ruleset/data/combat/situations.json`, with five called-shot locations. Each is
  modifiers on the attack target per range (`melee`, `ranged`), or a `forbids` that makes the
  attack an error. Cover carries origin `environment`, so two covers keep the stronger; a
  blinded attacker and an invisible target share the source `situation:unseen`. Called shots
  add to the attack target, multiply the damage dice before the crit, and list riders on a hit.
  Spec: `docs/core/combat.md#situations-and-called-shots`. 20 `combat` vectors and 1 `actions`
  vector. **[V 2026-11-06]**
- **Decision:** `resolution` now stacks its `add` modifiers by `docs/core/modifier.md` instead
  of summing them, so situations stack under the one rule. No earlier vector changed. **[V 2026-11-06]**
- **Decision:** difficult terrain is movement, not an attack term, and moves to an optional
  positioning module; standing up is the `stand_up` action (2 AP). **[A]**
- **Why:** the table and the called shots were prose in `docs/modules/combat.md`, each with its
  own stacking reading.
- **Open:** prone, blinded, and invisible are read from flags the caller passes; the
  `combat` prose fields on condition rows (`attack_penalty`, `defense_bonus`) are not read and
  now partly duplicate the situation rows **[U]**. Called-shot riders are listed, and the caller
  rolls the checks **[A]**. Range bands for called shots, the perks that halve their penalties,
  and Sharpshooter are not rows yet **[U]**.
