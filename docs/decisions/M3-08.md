# M3-08 Summon spells name defined creatures

Date: 2026-11-09

- **Decision:** a summon's `parameter` names a group in `summons/core.json`, and a group lists
  monster rows. Spec: `docs/modules/magic.md#summon-groups`. **[V 2026-11-09, `test_spells.py`]**
- **Decision:** one group per family, and a creature appears at its caster's level, resolved as
  a derived monster of its row, with element, power, and nature templates on top (the author's
  ruling, 2026-11-10; this replaces "a stronger spell summons more"). The spell's `magnitude`
  stays an authored count. **[V 2026-11-10, `test_spells.py`]** The caster picks the variant. **[A]**
- **Why:** the summons named a free word, so nothing said what appeared.
- **Difference from the issue:** every monster needs a drop row (`test_loot.py`), so the new
  familiars and elementals got one, copied from the giant rat and Fire Elemental. A summoned
  creature that vanishes should drop nothing; that is engine state, not a row. **[A]**
- **Reworked for the data model (2026-11-10):** the new rows have no numeric `id`, a group's
  variants are typed `monsterRef`, and `summon` has `parameter_category: summon`, so a plain word
  now fails validation. The elementals are built by the one composition. **[V 2026-11-10]**
- **Open:** growth moves attributes and HP only, so a summon's attack and ability amounts stay
  at its row's values at any level; check them against the balance bands. **[V 2026-11-10,
  read in `validate.py`; the balance effect is U]**
