# M3-08 Summon spells name defined creatures

Date: 2026-11-09

- **Decision:** a summon's `parameter` names a group in `summons/core.json`, and a group lists
  monster rows. Spec: `docs/modules/magic.md#summon-groups`. **[V 2026-11-09, `test_spells.py`]**
- **Decision:** one group per family, and no scaling: a stronger spell summons more for
  longer, and the creature keeps its row's stats. **[A]** The caster picks the variant. **[A]**
- **Why:** the summons named a free word, so nothing said what appeared.
- **Difference from the issue:** every monster needs a drop row (`test_loot.py`), so the new
  familiars and elementals got one, copied from the giant rat and Fire Elemental. A summoned
  creature that vanishes should drop nothing; that is engine state, not a row. **[A]**
- **Open:** a level-5 elemental at spell level 16 may be weak; check it against the balance
  bands when summons are played. **[U]**
