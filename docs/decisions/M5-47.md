# M5-47 Suggested builds: the frame, and the melee path

Date: 2026-11-16
Modified: 2026-11-16

- **Decision:** builds are a new collection, `builds` (`build.schema.json`), not new `concept`
  values in `examples/characters.json`: a build is a plan with no luck rolls and no gear log. A
  build holds the point-buy attributes, up to two creation perks, the tag skills, an ordered list
  of skill priorities, and a perk for every level from 2 to 30. **[V 2026-11-16, `validate.py`
  and `test_builds.py`]**
- **How legality is checked:** `build_reference.play` runs the kernel's own progression rules
  from creation to level 30 at the lowest luck roll (0), spends each pool down the priorities
  (points left over carry to the next level), and takes each perk through `take_perk`, so a legal
  build is legal for any roll. Race, background, gear, and skill modifiers from creation perks
  (Beast Phobia's +10 Animal Handling) are not read; they only add. **[A]** Points are spent
  before the level's perk. **[A]**
- **The melee sub-paths:** shield-bearer, two-handed fighter, and **duelist** in place of the
  issue's example "dual-wield": there are no dual-wield rules or perks yet (two weapons are an
  unbuilt `[A]` note in `docs/modules/combat.md`), so a dual-wield build would only be a duelist
  with a second blade it gets nothing for. **Gap.**
- **All three take Gifted.** A melee build needs four or five attributes at 6 to 8 for its perk
  prerequisites; Gifted's +1 to each is worth its ×0.9 pool. This says something about the perk
  table: its gates are wider than one point-buy can meet. **[A]**
- **The pages lean on M3-27** (the defense bands, the dodge and block stances), so the issue now
  needs M3-27.
- **Gaps: levels where the path has no perk worth taking.** The melee perk table holds about 25
  combat perks plus general ones; a build needs 29. Each build therefore names legal filler perks
  late, listed here instead of inventing perks:
  - **Shield-bearer:** 20 Quick Draw, 22 Sidestep, 25 Streetwise, 26 Meditative, 27 Night Eyes,
    29 Armored Evasion, 30 Brawler. Intimidating Presence (21) and Menacing (28) are worth
    something to a front-liner but cost 47 ranks of off-tag Intimidation.
  - **Two-handed fighter:** 21 Meditative, 22 and 23 Brawler, 25 Tracker, 26 Shield Training,
    27 Survivor, 28 Haggler, 29 Empath, 30 Mana Flow. The build also buys Blades 40 off-tag only
    for Power Attack, which every two-hander needs for Executioner.
  - **Duelist:** 24 and 25 Brawler, 27 and 28 Pack Mule, 29 Locksmith, 30 Light Fingers.
  - **What would fill them:** a second tier for the block, dodge, and two-handed ladders (as the
    bard and skald perks have), dual-wield perks, and perks that read heavy or light armor past 60.
    These belong to the content issues M6-02 and M6-03, not here.
- **Not done:** the magic path and the magic swordsman (M5-60), the ranged path and the two other
  hybrids (M5-61); their rows in the main page's table point at those issues.
