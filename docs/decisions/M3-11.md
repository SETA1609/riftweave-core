# M3-11 One rule set for turn-based and real-time play

Date: 2026-11-12

- **Decision:** one rule set on two clocks (`docs/core/actions.md#one-rule-set-two-clocks`). `stamina_per_ap` is 5 in `actions/core.json`: a real-time action costs its AP times 5 in stamina, and the `actions` command returns that as `stamina`. A duration lasts `ceil(duration / seconds_per_turn)` turns, and a tick covers the seconds that remain, so a 12-second effect totals the same over 12 seconds and over 3 turns. **[V 2026-11-12, `actions` and `consume` vectors in Python and Go]** The rate 5 is **[A]**.
- **Decision:** with `at_table`, `consume` turns a stamina restore into AP at 5 stamina to the point, keeps the remainder toward the next point, and holds the AP as `pending_ap` until the start of the next turn, when it joins the pool up to its `cap` of 10. A Stamina Potion's 6 AP therefore cannot pay for the turn it is drunk in. At a table the stamina pool itself does not rise. AP arrives only on a `turns` event; seconds passed with `advance` never start a turn. **[V 2026-11-12]** **[A]** for stamina not rising at a table.
- **Decision:** mana pays for all magic in both clocks; `cost.stamina` is gone from the spell schema. No spell used it. **[V 2026-11-12]**
- **Decision:** the 25 conditions lost `effects.ttrpg` and `effects.video_game`. Each states its rule once in `description` and as `modifiers` and its `combat` block. Seven `modifier_targets` rows are new and pending until a rule reads them: `checks`, `perception_checks`, `agility_checks`, `social_defense`, `incoming_damage`, `armor_dr`, `action_cost`. Petrified resists 20 of each of the 13 damage types and adds 100 poison and disease resistance; grappled gains `attack_penalty −10`; invisible gains `attack_penalty +10` and `defense_bonus −20`. **[V 2026-11-12, `conditions` vectors]**
- **Decision:** where the two old texts disagreed, the table text won, in d100 points: exposed is −10 evasion, armor DR ×0.5, damage taken ×1.25; slowed is speed ×0.5, −10 AGI checks, +1 AP on every action. Exposed's "−10 block" has no modifier, because `block` is both a skill and an action (M5-37). Exhaustion's modifier is its first level; the deeper levels are in its description, since a modifier does not scale with stacks yet. **[A]**
- **Decision:** the module docs keep one stamina formula, M2-08's `10 + END × 5 + level × 2` (the `sp` row); the old `15 + END × 5` text is gone, and Borin's stamina is 52.
- **Decision:** a new food, `fresh_apple` (1 HP a second for 12 seconds), gives the parity vectors a duration that does not divide by 5, because changing bread and cheese would have changed three food vectors.
- **Why:** the data carried per-mode columns and no conversion, so a table and a client could disagree, and a stamina restore could pay for its own turn.
- **Open:** whether a stamina restore at a table should also fill the stamina pool for a game that switches clocks mid-scene. Whether `stamina_per_ap` should be 5. **[A]**
