# M2-10 A rest ends food buffs unless you eat during it

Date: 2026-11-12

- **Decision:** every rest occasion has a `food` row: `clear` on `short_rest` and `long_rest`, `none` on `end_of_combat` (owner decision 2026-11-12). `clear` ends every running tick and held modifier whose origin is `core:origin/food`; a potion's or a spell's effect stays. Spec: `docs/core/rest.md#food-at-rest`. **[V 2026-11-12, `rest` vectors in Python and Go]**
- **Decision:** `rest` takes the running effects (`running`, the `ticks` and `modifiers` shapes `consume` returns) and an optional `meal`. It clears food by the row, then eats the meal through `consume`, so the meal's effects start when the rest ends and the same dish again runs once, fresh. A meal that is not `type: food` is refused. The rest does not advance the running effects by its minutes. **[V 2026-11-12]** **[A]** for not advancing them.
- **Decision:** the table shortcut (the GM settles what is left of a food buff after a fight, at once) is written in `docs/core/rest.md` and `docs/guide/gm.md`; the vectors keep the per-second rule (owner decision 2026-11-12).
- **Decision:** a meal at rest counts for the survival module's hunger meter through that module's table, once the module exists (owner decision 2026-11-12). Nothing in core reads it yet.
- **Why:** resting was free; a buff carried through any number of camps.
- **Open:** whether the rest should advance the running effects by its minutes, so a long meal buff ticks during the rest before the next meal. **[A]**
