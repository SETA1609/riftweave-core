# M3-13 Charisma strengthens summons through a perk

Date: 2026-11-12

- **Decision:** two perks, one per group, rather than one perk with a choice: `commanding_summons_magic` (INT, WIL) and `commanding_summons_physical` (STR, END, AGI). A perk row cannot hold a choice the player makes, and a second perk is how a caster gets the other group, as the issue allows. Each names its group as a ref, `summon_bonus: core:summon_bonus/<key>`, since a modifier holds only a number; the groups and the attribute that feeds them are data in `summons/bonuses.json`. [V 2026-11-12, `test_spells.py`]
- **Decision:** the bonus is the caster's attribute modifier (`formulas/attribute_modifier.json`, `CHA − 5`), floored at 0, added after the power and nature through the same merge as a template's `attributes`, so END moves an authored `hitPoints` by the `hp` row's `per_point` (8). A fire elemental at level 20 from a CHA 8 caster with the physical perk gains +3 STR, END, and AGI and 24 HP. [V 2026-11-12, `test_spells.py`]
- **Decision:** prerequisites level 6, CHA 6, and Orange or Black Magic 30, the two schools that summon (the familiar and elemental groups are orange, the undead group black). Each perk also gives +5 Persuasion or Intimidation, since every perk emits a modifier and two perks at one level do not emit the same targets. [A]
- **Why:** owner request, 2026-11-11: CHA should help summoning.
- **Open:** summoning has no kernel function or Go code yet: the creature is composed in Python (`monster_reference.summon`) and tested in `test_spells.py`, so there is no golden vector. A later issue that brings summoning into the kernel turns these tests into vectors. M3-14 gives the same bonus to companions and the party. [A]
- **Risk:** CHA 10 gives +5 on three attributes with both perks, on top of an ancient template; the balance pass (M5-09) should check a top summoner against the hits-to-kill bands.
