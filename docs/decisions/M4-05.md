# M4-05 Encounter and balance validation

Date: 2026-11-06

- **Decision:** the bands of `docs/modules/combat.md` §10 are rows in
  `ruleset/data/balance/core.json`: four tiers (levels, expected damage per hit, worn-set DR
  per armor category), the weapon dice band (1d6 to 2d10), and the hits-to-kill bands. Five
  checks read monsters and gear against them; each carries a `mode`, `gate` or `warn`, and a
  finding the owner accepts is a row with a reason. `balance_report.py --gate` runs in CI.
  Spec: `docs/core/balance.md`. **[V 2026-11-06]**
- **Decision:** `weapon_dice` and `armor_piece_dr` are gates; the dagger and the sling are
  accepted below the dice band as light weapons. `worn_set_dr`, `monster_damage`, and
  `monster_hits_to_kill` are warnings: the current data is outside them (18 findings). **[V 2026-11-06]**
- **Why:** the guidelines were prose, so data could drift from them unseen.
- **Open:** the per-tier DR columns of §10 (light 1 to 3 early, ...) are not read; the worn-set
  bands use the "total worn DR" line for every tier **[U]**. Monsters have no role, so all are
  read as standard **[A]**. The best medium set is 5 against 8 to 18 and the best heavy set 11
  against 14 to 28; the knight of example 1 (DR 22) needs materials the set check does not
  apply **[U]**. Monster damage runs about twice the tier band from level 4 up **[U]**.
- **Risk:** a warning nobody reads drifts like prose. M4-03 (bestiary) should fix the monster
  findings and turn both monster checks to `gate`.
