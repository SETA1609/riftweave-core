# M3-14 Charisma empowers allies: bard and skald perks

Date: 2026-11-15
Modified: 2026-11-15

- **Decision:** a new kernel function, `share` (`docs/core/share.md`): given the sharers near one
  ally, it emits per sharer within its perk's `range` one `core:origin/morale` modifier per
  attribute of the perk's group, worth `floor(max(CHA - 5, 0) × rank_share[rank - 1])`, lasting
  the perk's `turns`; morale stacks `highest`, so on each attribute the strongest counts.
  **[V 2026-11-15, `share.json` in Python and Go]**
- **Decision:** two perks after Leader: Bardic Inspiration (magic group, INT and WIL; a magic
  skill 45) and Skald's War Chant (physical group, STR, END, AGI; a weapon skill 45), level 18,
  CHA 7, two ranks, range 6, 3 turns. They reuse the M3-13 groups in `summons/bonuses.json`, so a
  bard and a summoner read CHA the same way. Each also emits +5 Persuasion or Intimidation, since
  every perk emits a modifier. **[A]** for the numbers.
- **Decision:** starting a share is the `inspire` action, 2 AP, so a perk that lowers its cost
  is a modifier on `inspire` like any action's. No such perk yet. **[A]**
- **Difference from the issue:** Leader is not rewritten. It stays a flat +5 on the pending
  `aura_ally_attack` target and becomes the ladder's first rung as the prerequisite of both
  share perks; turning its +5 into the CHA modifier needs an attack-roll share that no group
  holds, and the attack-roll target is being renamed (M5-37). **[A]**
- **Difference from the issue:** no session step. A session holds one actor and the kernel has
  no party, so the client or a later session issue calls `share` once per ally and passes the
  modifiers into that ally's commands, as the issue's owner decision allows. The frontend's
  generated API types are not regenerated, since nothing there calls `share` yet.
- **Risk:** a group of the same attribute lent by a bard and a summoner's own bonus stack, since
  the summon bonus is part of the creature and not a morale modifier; the balance pass (M5-09)
  should check a bard with a summoned party.
- **Decision (owner, 2026-11-15):** two tiers, as summoning has. Tier 1 (level 18) keeps its
  magic or weapon skill 45 and also needs a class skill, Persuasion 30 for the bard and
  Intimidation 30 for the skald; it lends (CHA − 5)/2 at rank 1 and CHA − 5 at rank 2. Tier 2
  (level 28), Bardic Anthem and Skald's Saga, needs the tier-1 perk at rank 2, CHA 8, and the
  class skill 60, and lends all of CHA − 5 over range 12 for 5 turns (`share.rank_share` [1]).
  Holding both is one morale bonus. **[V 2026-11-15, `share` and `progression` vectors in Python
  and Go]**
- **Decision:** a prerequisite perk may name its rank, `{ "perk": <ref>, "rank": N }`, the
  held-perk shape monsters use; a bare ref still means any rank. An unmet rank lists `min` and
  `has`. The validator, `test_perks.py`, and `test_characters.py` read both shapes.
