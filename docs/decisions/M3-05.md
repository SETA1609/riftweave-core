# M3-05 Spell delivery and save rolls for instant spells

Date: 2026-11-11

- **Decision:** an offensive spell is one with a harmful effect on another target, leaving out summons. All 32 name a delivery, from their effects' shape or `instant`. Spec: `docs/core/casting.md#delivery-and-saves`. **[V 2026-11-11, `test_spells.py`]**
- **Decision:** 11 spells are instant: those that reach the mind, body, or soul with nothing to dodge (Mind Spike, Psychic Scream, Charm Person, Mass Charm, Silence, Paralyze, Petrify, Sap Will, Mana Drain, Curse, Wither). Mental spells save on WIL, or on the Insight skill for charms; bodily ones on END. **[A]**
- **Decision:** the save target is the skill, or the attribute times 10. A failed save takes the effects, a margin under 30 halves magnitude and duration, and a margin of 30 or a crit stops them. **[A]** **[V 2026-11-11, `casting` vectors in Python and Go]**
- **Why:** an instant spell had no defense: it can't be dodged or blocked, and its save was unspecified.
- **Open:** the numbers are untested in play. A modifier on a save has no input yet, so no perk or item can change one. **[U]**
