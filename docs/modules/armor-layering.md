# Armor layering

Author: SETA1609

Optional module. Core does not require a skin layer to wear chain.

A layer is a tag on a torso, head, hands, or feet piece: skin, middle, upper, on_top. A recipe or a loader may refuse a middle piece with no skin piece. That refusal is this module. Unload it and chain still occupies the torso slot and still contributes DR.

Shields stay offhand. They are not a body layer.
