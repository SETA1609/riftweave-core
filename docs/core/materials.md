# Materials

Author: SETA1609

A material is an identity. A form is ore, ingot, fiber, textile, or stamp. A recipe is an edge between forms. An item is base + material + form.

Copper stays one row. It is not copied into copper_fiber. `draw_fiber` accepts the tags metal and plant. Granite does not qualify. Gold may be drawn and still be a bad sword because attack is 0.

`appliesTo` remains the old guard until recipe rows replace it. Do not delete it here.

Phase is a tag. A recipe must not require it. The wuxing module may read it.
