### GD_S19_07_ap-perks-traits-haste-slow Perks, creation traits, and conditions that change the AP pool
- Added: the Battle Tempo perk (level 12, +1 AP), the Heavy Handed (more melee damage, −1 AP) and Glass Frame (+1 AP, less HP) creation perks, and the hasted condition (+2 AP) with the haste effect.
- Changed: modifiers on `ap` change the `actions` command's `pool` as they do the derived `ap`, and both are kept between `pool.floor` (1) and `pool.cap` (10).
