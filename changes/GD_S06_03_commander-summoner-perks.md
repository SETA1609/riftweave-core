### GD_S06_03_commander-summoner-perks Commander summoner perks: the caster spends turns to drive their summons
- Added: the `command_summons` command (docs/core/commanding.md): an order (the new `command` action, 2 AP) gives the summons it reaches AP for their next turn, and a held command spell gives every summon a bonus to attack while held.
- Added: the commander perks Field Command, Sharp Orders, Voice of the Host, and Iron Drill (a `command` object on features), the held command spell Steady the Host, and the `command_summons` effect.
