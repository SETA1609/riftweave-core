### M5-49 Core skills for a lighter tabletop sheet
- Added: a sheet tracks only the skills a character has raised, and any other skill reads its starting value, `skill_start`, as a monster's does (`docs/core/progression.md`, the quick start, and the GM guide).
- Added: the optional grouped sheet. The `skill_groups` collection puts every skill in exactly one of 20 groups (one per magic color, armor untracked), with the `core:skill_group/<key>` ref; a member skill reads its start plus its group's ranks.
- Added: `skill_group_cost` on progression (`members_per_step` 2): a group rank costs the skill cost times `ceil(members / 2)`.
- Added: the `progression` command's `read_skill` op and `group` on `spend`, in Python and Go.
