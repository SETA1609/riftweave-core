### M3-24 Combat maneuvers: actions that apply effects for AP and stamina, not mana
- Added: the `maneuvers` collection, with Heavy Swing (one more AP, −10, ×1.5 damage, staggers) and Quick Strike (one AP less, half damage, a light weapon). The combat command takes `maneuver` and returns its AP, stamina, and effects.
- Added: the `maneuver` effect channel, checked by `validate.py`; `stagger` declares it.
- Added: `maneuvers` on monster rows and templates; the troll has Heavy Swing.
