### M5-35 A feature's bonus is written once
- Changed: a feature states its mechanics once, in `modifiers`; the ten creation traits (`gifted`, `genius`, the phobias, the allergies, and the rest) now emit modifiers, with the same source and origin as a perk.
- Removed: the `effects` list on features, and the seven effects only it used (`allergy`, `exotic_material_affinity`, `attack_speed`, `aimed_shots_restriction`, `skill_points_modifier`, `tag_skills_increase`, `carry_capacity_modifier`) **(breaking)**.
- Added: fourteen pending modifier targets for the traits: the phobia and allergy flags, `cannot_aim`, `exotic_material_affinity`, `magic_resistance`, `undead_resistance`, `ranged_attack_speed`, `skill_point_pool`, `starting_skill_points`, and `spell_absorption_chance`.
