### M3-16 Natural weapons are unarmed attacks
- Changed: every natural weapon rolls to hit with `unarmed` (`natural_weapons/core.json` names `skill`), and the unarmed perks (`unarmed_damage`, `unarmed_strike_damage`) change its damage; a creature with no attack slams.
- Changed: a feature's `natural_weapon` is a `core:natural_weapon/<key>` ref, sized by the actor's size; `catman_claws` names `claw` **(breaking)**.
