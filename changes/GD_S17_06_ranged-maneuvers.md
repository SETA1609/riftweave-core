### GD_S17_06_ranged-maneuvers Ranged maneuvers: the split nock, the held breath, pinning fire, and the trigger watch
- Added: the Split Nock, Held Breath, Pinning Shot, Pinning Fire, and Trigger Watch maneuvers, the Two on the String, Still Hunter, Heads Down (two tiers), and Watchful Aim perks, and the `pinned` effect.
- Added: the `overwatch` command for Pinning Fire and Trigger Watch, `attack_sequence`'s `maneuver`, and `combat`'s `moved`, `attacker.range_normal`, and `attacker.maneuvers`.
- Changed: a maneuver is refused when the given perks (or a monster's maneuvers) do not unlock it, or when it belongs to another command.
