### M3-10 Power and nature templates for summons and monsters
- Added: template slots (`element`, `power`, `nature`) and the templates `dire`, `elder`, `ancient`, `demonic`, `angelic`, and `otherworldly`, which add attributes, resistances, spells, and tags; generated monsters (a row plus a power and a nature) and the summon perks that unlock them through the new `unlocks` field.
- Added: the `natural_weapons` collection (dice by creature size), `ability` and `weapon` on monster attacks, `damagePerLevel` on creature growth, INT on elementals, and the effect `damage_poison`.
- Added: the example rows `dog`, `wolf`, `elder_wolf`, `demonic_hound`, and `undead_horse`; `dire_wolf` is now `wolf` plus `dire`.
- Removed: `damage.amount` on monster attacks; an attack rolls its weapon's dice plus its attribute modifier **(breaking)**.
- Changed: monster attributes have no maximum, and level growth no longer stops at 10.
- Changed: oozes and breath, bolt, and touch attacks are spell-like abilities that pay mana and the cast AP.
