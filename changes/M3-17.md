### M3-17 Monster attack and defense skills grow with level
- Added: `monster_skill_growth` in `progression/core.json`: a monster's unwritten combat and magic skills gain 17 per level above 1, up to 100, so a level-matched monster keeps within 15 points of a player's main skill; other skills stay at their starting value, and a written skill does not grow.
- Fixed: the two Commanding Summons modifiers name `operation`, as every modifier must since M5-46; `main` failed validation after M3-13 merged.
