### M4-07 Loot composes items: default materials and size
- Changed: a loot row's `material` lands only on items whose `default_materials` include it; any other item drops in its first default material, valued by the material that landed.
- Added: `size` on loot rows (`undersized`, `normal`, `oversized`): a weapon moves its dice one rung and its attack by ±10, and is valued at 75 or 150 percent; the loot result names a sized weapon's `size`, `dice`, and `attack`. Giants drop oversized weapons, the goblin skirmisher undersized ones.
- Changed: a row's overrides may list `default_materials`; the elven wood longbow is found only in elven wood.
