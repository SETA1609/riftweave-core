### M3-27 One attack roll: defense is a band, a shield is DR, block and dodge are declared stances
- Changed: an attack is one d100 roll; the defender's evasion is a band of the attacker's margin (half the Evasion skill), with no defender roll **(breaking)**.
- Changed: `defender.reaction` is replaced by `defender.stance` (`dodge` doubles the evasion band; `block` adds a band of half the shields skill and needs `shield_dr` and `shields_skill`); `reaction_tags` is gone from the result, which gains `evasion_rate`, `block_rate`, `stagger_ap`, and the `blocked` outcome **(breaking)**.
- Changed: a carried shield's `shield_dr` adds to armor DR against physical types with no action.
- Changed: Riposte and Bulwark read a window of their stance's band; Bulwark staggers the attacker for 2 AP; Spell Ward gives a block band of half the width against magic.
- Added: the band trees `evasion_rate`, `dodge_evasion_rate`, `block_rate`, `magic_block_rate`, and `bulwark_stagger_ap` in `combat/pipeline.json`, and 54 combat vectors for the bands.
