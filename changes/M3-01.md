### M3-01 Casting procedure: decide scope, then specify
- Added: `docs/core/casting.md`, `ruleset/data/casting/core.json` (`casting.schema.json`), and the `casting` command (gate, mana and reagent cost, one resolution on the color skill with an armor penalty, concentration, dispel) in Python and Go, with its vectors.

- Changed: `silenced` blocks every cast; verbal and somatic components, counterspell, and margin-scaled magnitudes are not core.
