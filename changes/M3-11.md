### M3-11 One rule set for turn-based and real-time play
- Added: `stamina_per_ap` (5) in `actions/core.json`; `actions` returns the real-time `stamina` cost, and `consume` with `at_table` turns a stamina restore into AP that arrives next turn, up to the pool cap.
- Added: seven pending modifier targets for condition rules (`checks`, `perception_checks`, `agility_checks`, `social_defense`, `incoming_damage`, `armor_dr`, `action_cost`), and the food `fresh_apple`.
- Removed: `effects.ttrpg` and `effects.video_game` on conditions, and `cost.stamina` on spells **(breaking)**.
- Changed: each condition states its rule once, in `description` and as modifiers; the module docs give one stamina formula, `10 + END × 5 + level × 2`.
