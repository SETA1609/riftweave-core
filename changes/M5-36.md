### M5-36 Composition bases are typed references
- Changed: a monster's `base` is `core:creature_type/<key>` or `core:monster/<key>`, an equipment row's is `core:weapon_base/<key>`, `core:armor_base/<key>`, or `core:accessory_base/<key>`, and every `default_materials` entry is `core:material/<key>` **(breaking)**.
- Added: the reference gate walks the base files and resolves these references.
