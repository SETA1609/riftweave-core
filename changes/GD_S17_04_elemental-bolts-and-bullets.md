### GD_S17_04_elemental-bolts-and-bullets Elemental bolts and bullets: a grenade's burst at the weapon's damage
- Added: Fire, Frost, Thunder, and Acid Bolts and Bullets, ammunition rows with a `burst_radius`.
- Added: `combat`'s `round` and `burst`: a round's damage type and riders apply to the shot, and each creature in the burst takes an area roll at the shot's damage roll and crit.
- Added: `validate.py` checks that only bolts and bullets burst and that their riders have the grenade channel.
