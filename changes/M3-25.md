### M3-25 Raise Horde and Conjured Legion call minions, not summons
- Changed: a summon spell writes a `summon` block (`group`, `duration`) instead of a `core:effect/summon` entry in `effects`, which may now be empty beside it **(breaking)**.
- Changed: Raise Horde and Conjured Legion are composed from Raise Thrall and Summon Elemental and call `1d3+1` minions (the caller passes `count_roll`), which join `caster.minions` outside the summon limit.
- Added: the `minion` monster template (slot `minion`, attributes −2, `hit_points_multiplier` 0.25); a minion takes no power, nature, or Commanding Summons bonus.
- Added: `count_roll` and `caster.minions` on the casting input, `minions` on the result, and eight casting vectors.
