### M3-02 Spell list to the content floor
- Changed: the issue now also covers spells that disarm traps, dispel arcane hazards, and unlock locks, and hazard kinds (`mechanical`, `natural`, `arcane`) that pick the detect and disarm skill; it needs M4-04.
- Added: 62 spells (70 in all, 5 to 9 per color, levels 1 to 30), among them Disarm Trap, Greater Disarm, Unlock, Greater Unlock, Dispel Ward, and Greater Dispel; the effects `damage_radiant`, `damage_necrotic`, `damage_psychic`, `damage_thunder`, `damage_acid`, `disarm_hazard`, and `unlock`; `ruleset/scripts/test_spells.py`.
- Added: hazard `nature` (`mechanical`, `natural`, `arcane`) and, for arcane hazards, `color`: an arcane hazard is detected and disarmed with its color's magic skill; the hazard command's `spell` op.
- Changed: `summon` has no default color; seven reserved effects are now used; a spell effect's parameter may name equipment (`core:weapon/<key>`).
