### M1-02 Combat pipeline as data, formulas, and vectors
- Added: `docs/core/combat.md` (the order of operations), `ruleset/data/combat/pipeline.json` (evasion, crit, and pierce trees), a `pipeline` object in `combat.schema.json`, a `formulaNode` definition in `schema.json` and a `var` formula leaf, and the `combat` golden vectors with a reference runner.
- Changed: `docs/modules/combat.md` points at the pipeline as the authority for the physical tree.
- Changed: evasion is a defender reaction rolled after a hit, contested on margin like block; the defender takes one reaction per attack, evade or block. The `combat` vectors take `defender.reaction` (`kind` `evade` or `block`) instead of `defender.block`, and report `evaded`.
