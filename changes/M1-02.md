### M1-02 Combat pipeline as data, formulas, and vectors
- Added: `docs/core/combat.md` (the order of operations), `ruleset/data/combat/pipeline.json` (crit and pierce trees), a `pipeline` object in `combat.schema.json`, a `formulaNode` definition in `schema.json` and a `var` formula leaf, and the `combat` golden vectors with a reference runner.
- Changed: `docs/modules/combat.md` points at the pipeline as the authority for the physical tree.
- Changed: evasion is a defender reaction rolled after a hit, contested on margin like block, against the raw evasion skill; the defender takes one reaction per attack, evade or block. The `combat` vectors take `defender.reaction` (`kind` `evade` or `block`) instead of `defender.block`, and report `evaded`.
- Changed: the attack's `delivery` decides the reaction: `single` is evaded whole, an evade halves `area` damage (`area_evade_damage` tree), and `instant` takes no evade or block. Evade is no longer limited to physical damage types; block is. Adds issue M3-05 for spell delivery and save rolls.
- Changed: evade and block rolls use the d100 resolution function: a reaction roll of 100 fumbles, and the defender's LCK crit window tags `crit` in `reaction_tags` (no effect yet).
