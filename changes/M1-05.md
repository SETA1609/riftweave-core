### M1-05 Modifier stacking and condition vectors
- Added: `docs/core/modifier.md` fixes the order (expire, dedupe, add, mul, set) and how conditions are applied and removed; `conditions` vectors for all 24 conditions and more `modifiers` vectors for every op.
- Changed: a modifier's `duration` is an enum (`turns`, `uses`, `while_equipped`, `while_applied`, `permanent`) with an optional `turns` count.
- Fixed: same-source modifiers on two different targets no longer replace each other.
