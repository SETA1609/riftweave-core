### M3-14 Charisma empowers allies: bard and skald perks
- Added: the `share` kernel function and command (`docs/core/share.md`): a bard or skald lends half, then all, of CHA − 5 to an ally's INT and WIL or STR, END, and AGI within 6 units for 3 turns, as a morale modifier, so two sharers keep only the stronger.
- Added: the perks Bardic Inspiration and Skald's War Chant (level 18, after Leader) and the `inspire` action (2 AP).
- Added: tier 2, Bardic Anthem and Skald's Saga (level 28, after the tier-1 perk at rank 2 and Persuasion or Intimidation 60): all of CHA − 5 over 12 units for 5 turns; tier 1 also needs Persuasion or Intimidation 30.
- Added: a prerequisite perk may name the rank it must be held at.
