### M3-13 Charisma strengthens summons through a perk
- Added: the Commanding Summons perks, `commanding_summons_magic` and `commanding_summons_physical`: a creature you summon adds your CHA − 5, never below 0, to its INT and WIL or to its STR, END, and AGI, after its power and nature.
- Added: the `summon_bonuses` collection (`summons/bonuses.json`), its `core:summon_bonus/<key>` ref, and `summon_bonus` on a feature.
