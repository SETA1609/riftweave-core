### GD_S06_02_bonded-summoner-perks Bonded summoner perks: an active summon empowers its caster
- Added: the `while_summoning` modifier duration, held only while the caster has an active summon; the cast drops it otherwise.
- Added: the bonded summoner perks Kindred Focus (+10 to every magic skill), Shared Wellspring (spells cost 20% less), and Elemental Kinship (+4 to a damage packet of an active summon's element, `bonded_element_damage`), and `caster.summon_templates` on the `casting` command.
