### GD_S19_04_ready-and-opportunity-attacks Opportunity attacks as reactions in the pipeline
- Added: `attack_sequence` runs `opportunity`, one melee attack of the loadout when a foe leaves reach without disengaging, a reaction at no AP; Disengage or no reaction left means no attack and nothing spent, and the result gives `fired` and `reason`.
- Added: reactions: everyone has `reactions_per_round` (1) between two turns, counted by the caller as `reactions_used` (`no_reaction_left` when spent); the perk Watchful Blade (level 6, AGI 7) grants `reactions_from_agi`, making it the AGI modifier, at least 1. In real time an engine fires opportunity attacks automatically or leaves them out.
- Changed: Phalanx adds a reaction a round (`add reactions_per_round 1`) instead of lowering an opportunity attack's AP; docs/modules/combat.md section 2 makes an opportunity attack a melee reaction, not 3 AP from the carried pool. Overwatch, the ranged attacker's held AP, is GD_S17_06's Trigger Watch.
- Removed: the `opportunity_attack_cost` modifier target, since an opportunity attack costs no AP (breaking).
