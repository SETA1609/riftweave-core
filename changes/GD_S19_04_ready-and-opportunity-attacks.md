### GD_S19_04_ready-and-opportunity-attacks Ready an attack on reserved AP, and opportunity attacks in the pipeline
- Added: the `ready` action holds the loadout's attack cost for a trigger, `enters_reach` or `acts_in_reach` (`readyTrigger` in schema.json, `triggers` on an action row).
- Added: `attack_sequence` runs `opportunity` (a reaction, no AP) and `readied` (paid from held AP, no reaction), one attack of the loadout each; Disengage, no reaction left, a trigger that did not happen, or too little held means no attack and nothing spent, and the result gives `fired`, `reason`, and `guard_lost`.
- Added: reactions: everyone has `reactions_per_round` (1) between two turns, each an opportunity attack at no AP, counted by the caller as `reactions_used` (`no_reaction_left` when spent); the perk Watchful Blade (level 6, AGI 7) grants `reactions_from_agi`, making it the AGI modifier, at least 1. In real time an engine fires opportunity attacks automatically or leaves them out.
- Changed: Phalanx adds a reaction a round (`add reactions_per_round 1`) instead of lowering the opportunity attack's AP; docs/modules/combat.md section 2 makes an opportunity attack a reaction, not 3 AP from the carried pool.
- Removed: the `opportunity_attack_cost` modifier target, since an opportunity attack costs no AP (breaking).
