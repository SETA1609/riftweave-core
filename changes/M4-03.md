### M4-03 Bestiary to the content floor
- Added: 37 monsters (48 in all) from level 1 to 30; monsters have `attacks`, `resist`, and `role`; every phase and damage type is used as an attack and as a resistance; `ruleset/scripts/test_bestiary.py` checks the floor; a drop row for each.
- Changed: the 11 older monsters' HP and damage are in their tier's bands; `monster_damage` and `monster_hits_to_kill` are gates and read the role; base growth caps an attribute at 10.
- Added: derived monsters: a monster's `base` may be another monster (`core:monster/<key>`), whose merged entry it inherits, with attributes and HP moved by the type's growth on a level change; `templates` on a row and `monsters/templates.json` with five element templates (`fire`, `frost`, `storm`, `acid`, `venom`) that set the phase, the attacks' damage type, resistance, tags, and a label prefix; the `ooze` creature type; the ooze, fire ooze, frost ooze, and giant.
- Changed: 13 monsters derive from a family's base monster (acid ooze from the ooze, the frost and storm giants from the giant, and so on); the frost giant's maul deals frost; monster names come from folklore, and `test_bestiary.py` rejects names from other games.
