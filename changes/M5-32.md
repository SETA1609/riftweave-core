### M5-32 Modifier targets are rows
- Added: `ruleset/data/modifier_targets/core.json` and `modifier_target.schema.json`: every modifier target that no other table holds, with its kind (`number` or `flag`) and `pending` while no golden vector uses it.
- Added: integrity gate 4 in `validate.py`: a modifier target names one row of the kind its op needs, every `modifier_targets` row is used, and `pending` matches the vectors.
- Changed: the factions module registers its 8 flags in its own `modifier_targets` file. A module whose modifiers name an unregistered target now fails validation.
