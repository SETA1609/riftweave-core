### GD_S05_03_defense-perk-ladders Defense perk ladders: shields, shield bash, evasion, no armor, and armor on casting
- Added: `rank_levels` (a level gate per tier) and `rank_values` (a value per tier) on features; `take_perk` gates each tier, and `validate.py` checks the gates and every build's tiers.
- Added: the tiered Shield Training, Shield Wall, Spell Ward, Guardian (pending), Boss Strike, Armored Evasion, Unarmored Grace, and Mailed Casting, and the Flowing Step perk (pending); the `block_band`, `spell_ward`, `boss_strike`, `unarmored_evasion_band`, `guardian`, and `flowing_step` targets.
- Added: the shield bash (`shield_bash` in `attack_sequences/core.json`; `attacker.shield_dr` and `after_block` inputs; `bash` and `stagger_ap` on a strike), the `shield_penalty` casting table and `caster.shield`, and `defender.armor` on `combat`.
- Changed (breaking): Spell Ward wards a magic hit in the block band (`warded`, `ward_percent`; `spell_ward_percent` and `spell_ward_table` in `pipeline.json`) instead of blocking it in a half band; `magic_block_rate` and `block_magic` are removed. Casting armor penalties are 0 / -5 / -15 / -30.
- Removed (breaking): the `acrobat` perk and `dodge_stamina`, `block_reduction`; `battle_mage` is `mailed_casting`. Bulwark needs Shield Training tier 2 instead of Spell Ward.
