### M2-05 Equipment to the content floor
- Added: 13 weapons (every weapon skill has three, across price tiers), 10 accessories, 15 consumables, and `equipment/gear.json` with tools and adventuring gear (`core:gear/<key>`); `docs/modules/equipment.md`; `ruleset/scripts/test_equipment.py`.
- Added: an optional `gear` block on equipment (`modifiers`, `uses`); a tool emits `add` on its skill, origin `equipment`.
- Fixed: the `crossbow` base names its skill.
- Changed: healing potions restore 20, 40, and 80 HP (were 50, 100, 200), the mana potion 15 (was 40), the stamina potion 30 (was 60); food heals over time, and a duration on a restore effect makes its magnitude per second.
- Added: weapon `size` (`undersized`, `normal` by default, `oversized`), one rung down or up `combat/dice_ladder.json`, with +10 or −10 to the attack; a sized weapon is composed from its base, not a row.
- Changed: every distance is in units (grid squares; 1.5 m for tabletop play): race speed, weapon range, area radius, and distances in descriptions.
- Changed: equipment bases list `default_materials` (was `valid_materials`): what a base is found and sold in, not a limit. Any material may be crafted onto any base.
- Changed: an applied effect's `duration` is whole seconds from 1, and 1, the default, is instant (was 0 or omitted); magnitude is per second.
