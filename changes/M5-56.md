### M5-56 A monster's effects, and the shields skill
- Changed: a monster's applied effects are `effects`, not `abilities` **(breaking)**.
- Changed: the shield skill is `core:skill/shields`, not `core:skill/block`; the block reaction reads modifiers on `shields` **(breaking)**.
- Added: a combat vector where a perk on `shields` turns a failed block into a block.
