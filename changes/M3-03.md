### M3-03 Cover, flanking, terrain, and called shots as modifiers
- Added: `ruleset/data/combat/situations.json` (`situation.schema.json`): nine situations (cover, flanking, prone, unseen, shooting into melee) and five called-shot locations as attack modifiers; the `stand_up` action; the `combat` command takes `range`, `situations`, `called_shot`, and attacker and defender `flags`, and returns `riders`.
- Changed: `resolution` stacks its attack modifiers by the one stacking rule instead of summing them.
- Changed: the Go core (`backend/`) reads `combat/situations.json` and takes the new `combat` inputs, and its `resolution` stacks attack modifiers the same way, so every vector passes in Go as well as in Python.
