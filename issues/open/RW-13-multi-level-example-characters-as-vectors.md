Single-level creation examples exist. Walk two characters (one martial, one caster) from creation to level 10, with perks, tag skills, gear, and spells, and encode them as vectors.

## Done when

- [ ] Two characters, each with a level-by-level record.
- [ ] Each level's derived stats match a vector.
- [ ] Prose in `character-creation.md` is generated from or checked against the record.
